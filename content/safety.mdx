description: "Open the product-specific pre-use checklist and emergency instructions before operating a device."
---

Use the guide for the product in front of you. Keep emergency instructions available on another device or print them before a session that may take place offline.

<Cards>
  <Card title="OSSM safety and emergency stop" href="/ossm/safety">
    Prevent pinch and impact injuries, verify Stop, and know the safe
    powered-off state.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lockbox safety and emergency access" href="/lockbox/safety">
    Practise access before locking and choose the correct software or hardware
    fallback.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deep Throat Trainer safety" href="/dtt/safety">
    Check breathing, positioning, toy compatibility, cleaning, and stop
    conditions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="RADR safety and Stop" href="/radr/safety">
    Begin at zero intensity and verify pause and full-stop behaviour on the
    target device.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="error" title="Stop and get help">

Stop using a product if it is damaged, behaves unexpectedly, cannot reach its expected safe state, or causes pain, numbness, dizziness, breathing difficulty, or distress. Disconnect power when it is safe to do so and email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com).

</Callout>
