Use the guide for the product in front of you. Each one starts with how to stop the device.

<Cards>
  <Card title="OSSM safety and emergency stop" href="/ossm/safety">
    Wired Remote: press and hold the right knob to stop. RADR: press the middle
    button on its control screen to pause. If anything seems wrong, unplug the
    power supply.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lockbox safety and emergency access" href="/lockbox/safety">
    Press **Lock/Enter** once (press again if the first press only woke the
    screen), then select **Emergency Unlock** and confirm. If software unlock
    is disabled, or the device is unresponsive or out of power, use the
    hardware backplate method. It is destructive, and the backplate must be
    replaced afterwards.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deep Throat Trainer safety and stopping" href="/dtt/safety">
    Pull back from the toy at any moment; you don't need to press anything.
    Then switch the Trainer off, and unplug its USB cable if it's connected.
  </Card>
  <Card title="RADR safety, pause and stop" href="/radr/safety">
    On a control screen, press the middle button to pause. Switching RADR off
    also stops a connected OSSM during play.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## If something goes wrong

1. Stop the device using the steps in its guide, or move away from it.
2. When it's safe to reach: unplug the OSSM's power supply; switch the Deep Throat Trainer off and unplug its USB cable; switch RADR off. For a Lockbox, follow its emergency access guide instead.
3. Tell us what happened (see [Report a safety problem](#report-a-safety-problem)).

**Stop and get help.** Stop using a product if it is damaged, behaves unexpectedly, can't reach its safe state, or causes pain, numbness, dizziness, breathing difficulty or distress. If you were hurt, get medical advice first.

**Don't use it again yet.** After a safety problem, don't use the product again until we've helped you work out what happened. (kb/reply-style.md section 8: never ask a customer to re-test a device that overheated, sparked or hurt someone.)

**Keep the guide handy.** If you might use a product without internet, save or print its safety guide first.

## Report a safety problem

Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com). Please include:

- which product it is
- what happened, and when
- the firmware version, if your device shows it on its start-up screen
- photos or a short video of the device and its power supply or charger. Please show only the device.
