<Callout type="info" title={"Use the RADR Web Flasher"}>

The RADR Web Flasher lets you install or recover firmware directly from your browser over a USB-C cable — no software to install. Open the [RADR Web Flasher](/radr/tools/web-flasher) in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.

In the meantime, most updates can be done over the air from your device: go to **Settings → Update Device**. See [Updating Your Device](/radr/guides/user-guide/updating-your-device) for step-by-step instructions.

If your RADR isn't responding and you need to recover it, [contact support](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) and we'll help you get back up and running.

</Callout>
