---
title: "Open the RADR Web Flasher"
description: "Open the working RADR browser flasher and review its USB, browser, recovery, and verification requirements."
---

The RADR Web Flasher has moved to the device's Tools section.

<Callout type="info" title="Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer">

Open the dedicated [RADR Web Flasher](/radr/tools/web-flasher) to install or recover firmware directly from your browser over USB-C.

</Callout>

Most updates can also be installed over the air from **Settings → Update Device**. See [Updating Your Device](/radr/guides/user-guide/updating-your-device) for details.
