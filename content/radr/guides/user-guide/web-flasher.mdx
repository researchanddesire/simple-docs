The RADR Web Flasher lets you install or recover firmware directly from your browser over a USB-C cable — no software to install.

<Callout type="info" title="Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer">

Web Serial is not available in Safari, Firefox, or mobile browsers. Use a data-capable USB-C cable and close any other app using the serial port.

</Callout>

<DeviceFlasher device="radr" />

Most updates can also be installed over the air from **Settings → Update Device**. See [Updating Your Device](/radr/guides/user-guide/updating-your-device) for details.
