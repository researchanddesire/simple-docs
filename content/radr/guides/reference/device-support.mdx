Use this page before buying or connecting a third-party device. A model appearing in an upstream Bluetooth registry does **not** mean Research and Desire has validated it with RADR.

<Callout type="warn" title="Start every new connection unloaded">

Keep the connected device clear of the body, start at its lowest output, and verify pause or Stop before use. Disconnect immediately if a control behaves differently from the label on RADR.

</Callout>

For upstream protocol coverage, consult the [Buttplug device list](https://iostindex.com/?filter0ButtplugSupport=4). That list describes upstream protocol support, not RADR validation.

## Verify a connection

1. Update only after reading [Update RADR](/radr/guides/user-guide/updating-your-device) and the connected device's release instructions.
2. Power on the target device and place it in its documented pairing or advertising mode.
3. Follow [Scan and Connect a Device](/radr/guides/user-guide/device-scanning).
4. With the device unloaded, test the lowest output first.
5. Test pause or Stop and confirm the physical device stops before continuing.
6. Test one supported control at a time. Do not assume an advanced feature is mapped.

## If a device is missing or behaves incorrectly

- Disconnect it and stop using the pairing until its behavior is understood.
- Record the RADR firmware version, exact brand and model, the other device's firmware version, and which control failed.
- Describe the expected and actual physical response. A short video is useful only when it can be recorded safely.
- Email [support@researchanddesire.com](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us) or use the [contact form](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us).

Compatibility ownership: the Research and Desire firmware/support team maintains this matrix. A reported device remains unvalidated until that team reproduces the result and updates this page.
