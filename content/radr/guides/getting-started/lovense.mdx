---
title: "Connect RADR to a Tested Lovense Device"
description: "Disconnect the Lovense app, connect a tested model to RADR, and verify low-intensity output and Stop."
---

1. Find the exact model in [Supported Devices](/radr/guides/reference/device-support). Do not assume similar model names have the same controls.
2. Charge the device and follow its manufacturer instructions for positioning, cleaning, and safe intensity.
3. Close the Lovense app and disconnect any other Bluetooth controller.
4. Set RADR output to zero and power on the Lovense device.
5. Power on RADR, select the exact device name, and wait for **Device ready!**.
6. Without body contact, increase to the lowest practical intensity for a moment.
7. Press the middle button and confirm output stops.

<Callout type="warn" title="Only documented capabilities are supported">

RADR may expose fewer controls than the Lovense app. Do not infer support for rotation, inflation, thrusting, heating, sensors, or independent motors unless the support table explicitly lists it.

</Callout>

If the device is absent from the list or its control mapping differs, turn it off and report the model, hardware revision, and app-reported firmware to support.
