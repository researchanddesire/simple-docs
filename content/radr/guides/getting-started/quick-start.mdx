description: "Charge RADR, prepare a supported target, scan, connect, and verify low output and Stop in about five minutes."
---

**Outcome:** RADR controls the intended target and its pause and Stop actions have been verified without body contact.

## Prerequisites

- Charged RADR and charged or powered target
- Target listed in [Supported Devices](/radr/guides/reference/device-support)
- Target's own safety instructions
- No phone app or other controller connected to the target

## Connect

1. Read [RADR safety, pause, and Stop](/radr/safety) and set all outputs to zero.
2. Prepare the target with the device-specific guide:
   - [OSSM](/radr/guides/getting-started/ossm)
   - [Tested Lovense device](/radr/guides/getting-started/lovense)
   - [Another supported device](/radr/guides/getting-started/other-device)
3. Move RADR near the target and turn it on. It scans automatically.
4. If needed, open the main menu and select **Device Search**.
5. Use the right knob to highlight the exact target name, then press the right button.
6. Wait for **Device ready!** and confirm the expected control screen.
7. Without body contact, apply the lowest practical output briefly, then verify Pause and full Stop.

<Callout type="success" title="Success">

The correct target is named on screen, minimum output responds predictably, Pause stops output, and full Stop leaves every documented output at its safe starting value.

</Callout>

If the target is missing, unexpected, or does not stop reliably, turn it off and use [Device scanning](/radr/guides/user-guide/device-scanning) or [RADR troubleshooting](/radr/faqs).
