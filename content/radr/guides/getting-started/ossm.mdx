---
title: "Connect RADR to OSSM"
description: "Prepare and home OSSM, connect RADR over Bluetooth, and verify pause and full Stop before use."
---

1. Complete the [OSSM safety checklist](/ossm/safety#mandatory-pre-use-checklist) and keep the motion path empty.
2. Confirm OSSM uses firmware 2.0.0 or later as required by the current RADR integration. If the version is older or unknown, contact support before flashing.
3. Power on OSSM and allow homing to finish.
4. Set RADR speed and motion values to zero, then power on RADR.
5. Select the OSSM in the scan list and wait for **Device ready!**.
6. Confirm the OSSM control screen appears.
7. With the travel path empty, apply minimum speed and a short depth.
8. Press the middle button once and confirm **Paused** with motion stopped.
9. Double-press the middle button and confirm the documented full-Stop values before positioning anyone.

![RADR OSSM play controls with zero speed, depth and adjustment tabs, Pause, Stop, and Patterns](<../../img/image (2).webp>)

Do not continue if the wrong OSSM connects, the carriage moves before commanded, the screen values do not match motion, or either Stop test fails.
