---
title: "Connect RADR to Another Supported Device"
description: "Check a non-OSSM, non-Lovense target's capability record, scan safely, and verify its documented control and Stop mapping."
---

1. Locate the exact device in [Supported Devices](/radr/guides/reference/device-support).
2. Record the tested firmware, capabilities, limitations, and last-tested date. If any are missing, treat compatibility as unverified and contact support.
3. Follow the target manufacturer's charging, inspection, positioning, cleaning, and safety instructions.
4. Close every other app or controller that may hold the Bluetooth connection.
5. Set RADR output to zero, power on the target, then scan from RADR.
6. Select only an exact, expected device name.
7. Without body contact, test the documented control at minimum output and verify Stop.

<Callout type="error" title="Unlisted does not mean compatible">

Do not test an unlisted device on a person. Bluetooth discovery alone does not prove a correct or safe control mapping.

</Callout>

Use [Device scanning](/radr/guides/user-guide/device-scanning) for connection symptoms. Report unsupported or changed behaviour to [support@researchanddesire.com](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us).
