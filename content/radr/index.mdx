## Before any output

1. Read [RADR safety, pause, and Stop](/radr/safety).
2. Charge RADR and the target device.
3. Inspect the target device and its attachment or positioning.
4. Close other apps or controllers that may hold the Bluetooth connection.
5. Connect with every output value at zero.
6. Verify pause and full Stop before placing the target device in use.
