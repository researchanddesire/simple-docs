## Where should I start?

Use the [RADR start guide](/radr) to choose OSSM, validated Lovense, or another-device guidance. Before connecting anything, read [RADR Safety](/radr/safety) and test Stop with the target device unloaded.

## What do the bumper buttons do?

While controlling OSSM, the bumpers move between depth, sensation, and stroke adjustments. Their action is context-dependent; use [RADR Navigation and Controls](/radr/guides/user-guide/navigation-and-controls) for the complete button map.

## Why does a device not appear or connect?

Close other Bluetooth controllers, confirm the exact model is validated, put it in advertising mode, move it nearer, power-cycle both devices, and scan again. Do not select an unnamed result. Follow [Scan and Connect a Device](/radr/guides/user-guide/device-scanning) for the failure checklist.

## Is my device or firmware supported?

Use the dated [RADR Device Compatibility](/radr/guides/reference/device-support) matrix. Board-family names, an upstream Bluetooth registry entry, or a successful scan are not sufficient evidence that every control has been tested. Report the exact RADR version, target-device version, model, and behaviour when requesting support.

## How do I connect Wi-Fi?

RADR uses a temporary setup network to save a 2.4 GHz network for updates. The canonical steps and recovery advice are in [Connect RADR to Wi-Fi](/radr/guides/user-guide/connecting-to-wifi).

## How do I update or recover RADR?

Use [Update RADR Firmware](/radr/guides/user-guide/updating-your-device) for preparation, verification, and interruption recovery. If over-the-air update is unavailable or RADR will not start normally, review the data-cable and browser requirements before opening the [RADR Web Flasher](/radr/tools/web-flasher).

## How do I charge RADR?

Connect its USB-C port to a suitable USB power source with an undamaged cable. Battery runtime varies with display, feedback, radio use, battery age, and firmware; this documentation does not promise a fixed duration. Stop using a battery that swells, leaks, overheats, or is physically damaged and contact support.

## A button feels different, rattles, or does not register. Should I open RADR?

Do not open or adjust the enclosure based only on a generic FAQ. Record which control fails, whether the action registers on screen, and any recent drop or damage, then ask support for the correct inspection or replacement path.

## How do I get help or request compatibility?

Email [support@researchanddesire.com](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us) with the RADR firmware version, exact connected-device model and firmware, steps to reproduce, on-screen message, and actual physical response. You can also use the [contact form](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us).
