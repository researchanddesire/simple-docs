# A device does not appear

- Charge RADR and restart it near the device.
- Power on the device and let it finish starting.
- Disconnect every other remote, app, or browser controller.
- For OSSM, confirm firmware version 2.0.0 or later.

# RADR connects but motion does not start

Make sure the device completed its startup or homing process. Return the speed control to zero, check the display for the active mode, and then increase speed slowly.

# The connection drops

Stop the session. Move RADR closer, restart both devices, and reconnect before resuming. Do not bypass a stop or safety state.

Still stuck? Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with the RADR firmware version, connected product, and what both displays show.
