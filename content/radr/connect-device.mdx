# Connect

1. Power on the device and wait until it is ready.
2. Make sure no other controller or app is connected.
3. Power on RADR and wait for its five-second scan. Or choose **Device Search** from the main menu.
4. Turn the right knob to highlight a device and press the right button to connect. Press the left button to cancel.
5. Wait for the connected screen before changing any control.

RADR can automatically reconnect to a previously used device when it is nearby and available. It connects to one device at a time; return to the main menu before switching devices.

If the connection is lost during use, RADR and the supported device reduce or stop motion as designed. Resolve the connection before restarting.
