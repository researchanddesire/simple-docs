# Main controls

- **Left knob:** change speed.
- **Right knob:** change the active parameter, such as depth.
- **Middle button:** press once to pause; press twice to stop and reset the connected device.
- **Right button:** open a menu or confirm a choice.
- **Left button:** go back or return home.
- **Bumper buttons:** move between available modes or parameters.

The display shows which parameter the right knob currently controls. Available modes and parameters depend on the connected device.

In menus, turn the right knob to move through choices and press the right button to select. Press the left button to go back. With an OSSM connected, the bumpers move between **Depth**, **Sensation**, and **Stroke**.

Start at zero speed. Increase speed and depth gradually, one control at a time. Keep RADR in your hand or within immediate reach whenever the connected device can move.
