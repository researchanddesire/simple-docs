# Before you start

1. Charge RADR using USB-C.
2. Power on the device you want to control and let it finish starting.
3. Disconnect any other remote, app, or browser controller from that device.
4. Power on RADR.

RADR scans for nearby supported devices for about five seconds. Choose your device from the list on its display. If the device was powered on after that scan, open **Device Search** to scan again.

For OSSM, use firmware version 2.0.0 or later. Only one controller or app can be connected to a device at a time.

After connecting, begin at zero speed and review [RADR Controls](/radr/controls) before starting motion.
