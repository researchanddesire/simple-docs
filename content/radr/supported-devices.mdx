# R+D devices

RADR supports compatible R+D devices advertised for RADR control. For OSSM, firmware version 2.0.0 or later is required.

OSSM has full RADR control for speed, depth, sensation, stroke, and patterns. Lovense Domi and Domi 2 have direct vibration control. Support and available controls for other third-party models vary by RADR firmware.

The device must be powered on, nearby, and not connected to another remote, app, or browser controller.

The device list and available controls shown on RADR are authoritative for the installed firmware. A device appearing in a compatibility registry does not mean every feature has been tested. Begin at the lowest intensity and confirm basic controls before a session.

Developing an integration? Use the [Developer Docs](https://dev.researchanddesire.com/integrations) rather than the consumer setup steps here.
