---
title: "E‑LOCK‑14: Dashboard Sign‑In Failed"
description: "Error E‑LOCK‑14 means your Lockbox joined Wi‑Fi but could not finish signing in to the R+D dashboard after three attempts. Until it signs in, it can't receive commands from the dashboard."
---

On screen: "The Lockbox could not finish signing in to the dashboard. Check your internet connection, then retry, or skip for now."

Before the Lockbox connects to the R+D messaging server, it signs in to the dashboard to get its connection details. This error means that sign‑in did not complete, so the Lockbox never tried the messaging server.

## Common causes

- Slow or unstable internet — The dashboard's answer did not arrive in time
- Network filtering — Hotel, office, school and VPN networks can slow down or block the connection
- Temporary dashboard problem — The dashboard could not complete the request

## How to fix it

### Step 1: Check the internet connection

On a phone or laptop connected to the same Wi‑Fi network, open [dashboard.researchanddesire.com](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com). If it doesn't load, restart your router and wait 2 minutes.

### Step 2: Retry

Press the right button to **Retry**. The left button (**Skip**) starts the connection check again from Wi‑Fi.

### Step 3: Try a different network

Connect the Lockbox to a phone hotspot. If it signs in there, your usual network is slowing down or blocking the connection. See [Connectivity Troubleshooting](/lockbox/support/connectivity-troubleshooting).

<Callout type="info" title={"Info"}>

After setup is complete, the Lockbox doesn't stop on this screen: it keeps working offline and retries the sign‑in in the background.

</Callout>

## Confirm it’s fixed

<Callout type="success" title={"Success"}>

The Lockbox continues setup, and the dashboard shows the device online after a brief delay.

</Callout>
