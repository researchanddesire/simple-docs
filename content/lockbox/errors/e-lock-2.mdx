---
title: "E-Lock 2: Could Not Unlock"
description: "Get emergency key access first, then diagnose a Lockbox that cannot confirm it unlocked."
---

E-Lock 2 means the Lockbox attempted to unlock but could not confirm that the locking paddle reached the unlocked position.

<Callout type="warn" title="Access first">

If the key is needed urgently, do not work through routine retries. Use [Software Emergency Unlock](/lockbox/device-states/emergency-unlock) if available, then [Hardware Backplate Emergency Access](/lockbox/support/emergency-backplate). Contact local emergency services if access is still not possible.

</Callout>

## If access is not urgent

![Lockbox paddle and backplate orientation](../quick-start/images/physical-lock-inverted-annotated.webp)

1. Keep the Lockbox on stable power.
2. Remove external pressure from the lid/backplate and make sure nothing is loading the mechanism.
3. Attempt one normal unlock.
4. If the device is responsive but still reports E-Lock 2, try one documented [restart](/lockbox/device-states/restarting).
5. Do not repeatedly cycle or force the paddle.

## Stop and escalate

Use hardware emergency access and contact support if the mechanism binds, the device repeats E-Lock 2, the motor sounds abnormal, or the lid/backplate is damaged. Send [support@researchanddesire.com](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us) the code, current lock state, a photo, and the actions already attempted.
