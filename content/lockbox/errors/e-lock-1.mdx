---
title: "E-Lock 1: Could Not Lock"
description: "Clear a Lockbox locking obstruction, verify lid and paddle alignment, and know when to stop retrying."
---

E-Lock 1 means the Lockbox attempted to lock but its sensors could not confirm the locking paddle reached the locked position.

<Callout type="warn">

Treat the Lockbox as **unlocked**. Do not leave a key inside or begin a session until a test lock completes successfully.

</Callout>

## Inspect the mechanism

![Lockbox paddle orientation and backplate relationship](../quick-start/images/physical-lock-inverted-annotated.webp)

1. Remove the lid/backplate and key.
2. Check that nothing obstructs the locking paddle.
3. Move the paddle gently through its normal free movement. Do not force it.
4. Reseat the lid/backplate so it sits flush.
5. Attempt one unlocked test cycle.

<Callout type="success">

The error clears only when the device reports locked and the lid/backplate is secured. End the test and verify it also unlocks normally before starting a session.

</Callout>

## Stop and contact support

Stop retrying if the paddle binds, the enclosure or backplate is damaged, the motor makes an unusual sound, or E-Lock 1 returns after reseating. Keep the key outside the Lockbox and email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with a photo of the mechanism.
