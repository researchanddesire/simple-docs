---
title: "Using Lockbox Features"
description: "Choose a Lockbox feature and understand its roles, visibility, and safety implications."
---

Use these features only after completing a short test lock and reviewing [Lockbox Safety](/lockbox/safety).

| Feature                                           | Purpose                                        | Important boundary                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [Take a Break](/lockbox/using/take-a-break)       | Temporary key access during an active session  | Health and emergency access take priority over schedules or consequences |
| [Hide Remaining Time](/lockbox/using/hiding-time) | Hide the countdown from the Lockee             | Safety and emergency instructions must remain available                  |
| [Public Voting](/lockbox/using/public-voting)     | Let people with a shared link affect a session | Review audience, limits, and how to disable sharing first                |
| [Status Symbols](/lockbox/using/symbols)          | Interpret lock, network, and battery state     | A Wi-Fi icon alone does not confirm dashboard connectivity               |
