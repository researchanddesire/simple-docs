Learn what each icon on your lockbox display means, including lock status, Wi-Fi connectivity, and battery level.
**[Emergency unlock](/lockbox/using/emergency-unlock)**

## Social features
**[Public voting](/lockbox/using/public-voting)**

Let others vote to increase or decrease your lock time for added excitement and community engagement.

## Quick reference

### Lock session controls

During an active session, you can:

- **View status** — Check remaining time, lock state, and connectivity on the device display
- **Take breaks** — Pause your session temporarily (if enabled by your keyholder)
- **Emergency unlock** — Access your key immediately (triggers a 24-hour lockout)

<Callout type="info">

Your keyholder controls which features are available during your session. Some options may be disabled based on your session settings.

</Callout>
### When you need help

If something isn't working as expected:

1. Check the [status icons](/lockbox/using/symbols) to diagnose connectivity or lock issues
2. Use the [emergency unlock](/lockbox/using/emergency-unlock) if you need immediate access
3. Visit the [support section](/lockbox/support) for troubleshooting guides

<Callout type="warn">

Always familiarize yourself with the emergency unlock procedures before starting a session. Having a backup plan ensures you can access your key in any situation.

</Callout>
