## Before you start

The person who will be locked (the wearer) needs:

- A Chaster account and an R+D Dashboard account.
- A Lockbox connected to **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi** and paired to your R+D account. New box? Follow the [Lockbox Quick Start](/lockbox) first.
- An empty Lockbox for your first test.

A Chaster keyholder needs only a Chaster account. See [Who does what](#who-does-what).

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep independent emergency access">

Before storing keys, read the [emergency unlock guide](/lockbox/using/emergency-unlock) and [emergency backplate guide](/lockbox/support/emergency-backplate). Confirm that the empty-box test locks and releases successfully.

</Callout>

## 1 Choose the R+D Lockbox in Chaster

Create or join a lock in [Chaster](https://chaster.app/). Under **Locking method → Smart lockbox**, choose **Research And Desire Lockbox**.

<Callout type="info" title="A locking method, not an extension">

**Research And Desire Lockbox** is a locking method. You will not find it in Chaster's list of extensions. Choose it in **Locking method** when you create or join the lock.

</Callout>

![Chaster locking methods with Research And Desire Lockbox under Smart lockbox](./img/01-choose-locking-method.png)

Select **Link Account** in the setup panel to sign in to R+D.

![R+D Lockbox setup panel showing Link Account and the pairing check](./img/02-link-account.png)

## 2 Connect your Chaster account

1. In Dashboard, open **Integrations → Chaster → Connect Chaster**.
2. Sign in to Chaster if asked.
3. Review the permissions for **Research And Desire**, then select **Allow**.
4. Back in Dashboard, check **Connected** and the intended **Linked Account**.

![R+D Integrations page showing Chaster as Not connected and the Connect Chaster button](./img/03-connect-chaster.png)

![Chaster permission screen with the requested account access and Allow button](./img/04-authorize-account.png)

## 3 Confirm account linking and pairing

Return to Chaster. **Link your Account** and **Pair a Lockbox** should both be green. Select **Continue**.

If pairing is incomplete, [pair the Lockbox](/lockbox/quick-start/pairing) to the same R+D account. If the checks have not updated, reload the **Locking method** panel.

## 4 Start the empty-box test

On **Summary**, check the duration, timer visibility, and **Research And Desire Lockbox** locking method.

<Callout type="warning" title="Use a short test with a finite maximum">

Set a short duration and enable **Limit lock time** for the test. **No maximum lock time** can allow a misconfigured lock to run indefinitely. See [Chaster's explanation](https://docs.chaster.app/unlock-your-lock/#limit-lock-time).

</Callout>

1. Select **Start the lock**. The Lockbox shows the lock as an offer. The lock waits on Chaster until you accept it.
2. Leave the drawer empty for this test, close it, and press **Accept** on the Lockbox. The timer starts.
3. Confirm that the intended drawer has physically locked.

**You’re all set!** and a running timer do not confirm physical locking. Chaster has no Lockbox picker; if you own several boxes, check which one received the test.

If the Lockbox is already holding a lock, the new Chaster lock waits. Nothing is unlocked. The new lock is offered on the box when the running one ends.

![Chaster Summary showing the R+D Lockbox and the maximum-time warning](./img/07-review-and-start.png)

*This screenshot shows the no-maximum warning. Set a finite maximum for your test.*

## 5 Finish the test

1. When Chaster allows unlocking, unlock on Chaster, or use **Unlock** on the Dashboard or the Lockbox.
2. Follow any device prompts.
3. Confirm that the drawer opens, the screen says unlocked, and both Chaster and Dashboard show the session has ended.

<Callout type="warning" title="Chaster decides when the lock ends">

While a Chaster task or extension, a minimum duration, or a freeze still blocks unlocking, or Chaster's own timer is still running, the box stays locked. Dashboard and the Lockbox show **Waiting for Chaster** with the reason. The R+D timer reaching zero does not open the box on its own. See the [unlock FAQ](/lockbox/chaster/faq#why-will-the-lockbox-not-open-when-the-timer-reaches-zero).

</Callout>

For a later session with keys, put them inside and close the drawer before you press **Accept** on the Lockbox.

## Who does what

| Person | Needs | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wearer | An R+D account connected to Chaster and a paired Lockbox | Accepts each new lock on the Lockbox. Returns the keys and relocks on the Lockbox after a temporary opening. |
| Chaster keyholder | A Chaster account only | Runs the lock from Chaster: time changes, freezes, hiding or showing the timer, temporary openings, and unlocking all reach the Lockbox. |

If a keyholder creates the lock for you, you still connect your own R+D account and pair your own Lockbox, then accept the lock on the box. See the [keyholder FAQ](/lockbox/chaster/faq#keyholders).
