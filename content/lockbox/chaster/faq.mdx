## Starting a lock

### Where do I find Research And Desire in Chaster?

It is a **locking method**, not an extension, so it is not in Chaster's extension list. When you create or join a lock, open **Locking method → Smart lockbox** and choose **Research And Desire Lockbox**. See [Setup](/lockbox/chaster/setup).

### When does the Lockbox lock?

**Start the lock** sends the lock to the box as an offer, and the lock waits on Chaster. Put the key in, close the drawer, and press **Accept** on the Lockbox. The timer starts once you accept. Confirm that the intended drawer has locked. A delay is possible; no fixed response time is verified.

### Does the ready confirmation mean my Lockbox is physically locked?

No. It confirms a linked account and paired box, not connectivity, drawer closure, or physical locking.

### Where can I see my Lockbox during the session?

Open **Actions → Research And Desire Lockbox** in Chaster. Its card shows reported status and may lag behind changes. Select **Open lockbox page** for Dashboard controls. Chaster's timer visibility rules still apply.

### What happens if my Lockbox is already running a lock?

A new Chaster lock does not replace a running R+D session. It waits on Chaster, and its Chaster history says it is waiting for the Lockbox. Nothing is unlocked. When the running session ends, the waiting lock is offered on the box. Only an offer you have not accepted yet is replaced by a newer lock.

### Can I move a running Chaster lock to my Lockbox?

Yes, if the lock uses **Research And Desire Lockbox**. Connect your R+D account and pair your Lockbox, then open **Integrations** in Dashboard and choose **Pull from Chaster**. The box receives the time Chaster has left, not the lock's full original duration. A hidden timer stays hidden and a frozen lock stays frozen. Accept the lock on the box to start it.

### My keyholder created the lock for me. What do I need?

Your own R+D account connected to Chaster, and your own paired Lockbox. Then accept the lock on the box. Your keyholder needs neither. The **Research And Desire Lockbox** page on the Chaster lock tells each of you what is still missing.

## Unlocking

### Why will the Lockbox not open when the timer reaches zero?

Chaster decides when the lock ends. While a Chaster task or extension, a minimum duration, or a freeze still blocks unlocking, or Chaster's own timer is still running, the box and Dashboard stay locked. Dashboard shows **Waiting for Chaster** with the reason, for example *Tasks pending*. The Lockbox shows its **Frozen by Chaster** screen with the same reason.

The R+D timer reaching zero does not open the box or end the Chaster lock. As soon as Chaster allows unlocking, the wait ends. Unlock on Chaster, Dashboard, or the box, then confirm that the drawer opens and both sessions have ended.

Software emergency unlock works as usual. A box that is offline when its timer ends, or runs old firmware, may still open on its own timer. See [Integration limitations](/lockbox/chaster/limitations#status-and-timers).

### What does Unlock on the Dashboard do for a Chaster lock?

It asks Chaster to unlock. If Chaster agrees, both sessions end and the Lockbox opens. If Chaster refuses, nothing changes: the box stays locked and Dashboard says what Chaster is waiting for. On a lock with a Chaster keyholder, the wearer can use **Unlock** only once the lock is ready to unlock; until then, the keyholder unlocks from Chaster.

### Why is my timer frozen?

Check Chaster's freeze status. A keyholder or extension can pause the countdown. R+D also freezes a new lock while it waits for you to accept it on the box, and accepting releases that freeze. If the lock ends before you accept it (declined, timed out, abandoned, or replaced), R+D removes its own freeze from the Chaster lock. Freezes placed by a keyholder or another extension stay.

If the reason starts with **Waiting for Chaster**, see [the unlock answer above](#why-will-the-lockbox-not-open-when-the-timer-reaches-zero).

### Does hiding the timer on Chaster reach the Lockbox?

Yes. Hiding or showing the timer on Chaster updates Dashboard and the box within moments, without **Sync** or a restart. A box that was offline catches up when it reconnects.

## Breaks and hygiene openings

### How do I take a temporary opening or hygiene break?

The lock needs Chaster's **Hygiene opening** extension. Then start an opening on Chaster, choose **Take a Break** on the Lockbox, or choose **Break** in Dashboard. R+D asks Chaster first, and Chaster's rules decide: keyholder-only openings, openings left, cooldown, and verification picture. The cooldown can block the first opening too.

When the opening starts, the box opens. Return the key, close the drawer, and press **End Break** on the Lockbox before the opening runs out. Ending the break on the box resumes the Chaster lock.

### What happens if Chaster refuses my break?

- **From Dashboard:** nothing is recorded, the box stays locked, and Dashboard shows Chaster's reason.
- **Keyholder-only openings:** the Lockbox refuses on its own screen.
- **No openings left, or a verification picture required:** current firmware opens first. The box then shows *Chaster is asking you to end your break now*, and Dashboard shows **Break refused by Chaster** with the reason. Return the key, close the drawer, and press **Accept**. The lock continues.

### What happens if I return late from a break?

Chaster's **Hygiene opening** extension applies its own late-return penalty. The Lockbox does not add a second one. If Chaster never granted that opening, for example because it refused it, the Lockbox's own break penalty applies once.

### Why is the Lockbox waiting after I end a break in Chaster?

If the opening ends on Chaster while the drawer is open, the box asks you to close it. Return the key, close the drawer, and follow the Lockbox's prompts. If the box was already locked, the break simply finishes. If the timer stays paused, check Chaster for a separate freeze.

## Keyholders

### Does my Chaster keyholder need an R+D account?

No. Everything a keyholder does on Chaster reaches the Lockbox: adding or removing time, freezing and unfreezing, hiding or showing the timer, temporary openings, and unlocking. Linking an R+D account is optional.

### What does the wearer still have to do?

- Have an R+D account connected to Chaster, and a paired Lockbox.
- Accept each new lock on the Lockbox.
- After a temporary opening, return the keys and relock on the Lockbox. Chaster has no remote relock.

### What is only available in Dashboard?

R+D voting links, lock history and statistics, Lockbox settings and firmware updates, emergency unlock settings, and **Pull from Chaster**.

### Can a keyholder open the box just once?

Yes. Add **Hygiene opening** to the running lock on Chaster (you can allow only the keyholder to open it), start the opening, then remove the extension again. Each step reaches the Lockbox. The wearer returns the keys and relocks on the box as usual.

### Can a lock started on Chaster get an R+D keyholder?

No. A lock started on Chaster takes its keyholder from Chaster. To use an R+D keyholder, have them start the lock from Dashboard instead; that creates the Chaster lock too.

## Sync and Pull

### Do time changes stay in sync?

Supported time changes sync in both directions. After an error, check both deadlines before retrying: Dashboard may have changed even if Chaster rejected the adjustment.

### What does Pull from Chaster do?

It previews the Chaster changes before applying them. Check the selected lock, current Dashboard session, and proposed action; Pull can start, update, replace, or end a session. Opening the preview applies nothing.

### Why does Pull offer to override or end my current session?

- **Override current session** replaces the Dashboard session with the selected Chaster lock.
- **End** means Chaster reports no active locked session while Dashboard still has one.

Check both platforms before confirming. If anything changes, refresh the preview. With several Chaster locks, Pull uses the linked lock when possible or asks you to choose.

### Who can apply changes through Pull?

The R+D account owner or a keyholder assigned to the current R+D session. **Give Up Control** blocks the owner from applying changes. Only the owner can connect their Chaster account.

### Why can Pull not use my Chaster lock?

Check that the lock uses **Research And Desire Lockbox** and has an available integration session. Creating or replacing a session may also require timing information.

Reconnect with **Connect Chaster** if authorization expired. For an expired state token, return to **Integrations** and start a fresh connection attempt.

### Why does Pull say already in sync when timers differ?

Pull checks only supported session state and settings. Refresh both pages and compare matching time values and time zones. Chaster's maximum deadline may differ from its current countdown. See [Integration limitations](/lockbox/chaster/limitations).

## Voting

Follow [Voting with Chaster](/lockbox/chaster/voting) for setup and link-sharing steps.

### Does Chaster voting enable Dashboard voting?

No. Dashboard voting starts **off** for locks created from Chaster, so share Chaster's **Share links** page instead. Pull does not copy the voting switch or vote sizes. Users with **Modify session** access can configure Dashboard voting separately.

### Do Dashboard votes count toward Chaster's required visits?

No. Use Chaster's **Share links** link for required visits. Dashboard sends time adjustments, and each platform keeps its own vote records.

### What if a Dashboard vote reports an error?

Check both deadlines before retrying. The vote and Dashboard deadline change may remain recorded even if Chaster rejects the adjustment.

### Does hiding the public listing stop voting?

No. **Show in Public Links** hides the directory entry. To restrict ordinary public access, turn off **Enable public voting on lock duration** in the current session and save. Chaster Share links remain separate.

## Accounts

### Why is the account check green in Chaster but the dashboard says Not connected?

Complete **Connect Chaster** in Dashboard and check **Linked Account**. Chaster's account check recognizes the account association; Dashboard also checks authorization. Reload Chaster's **Locking method** panel if its checks have not updated.

### Why does Chaster say no Lockbox is linked?

Pair the box to the R+D account linked to Chaster. Follow the [pairing guide](/lockbox/quick-start/pairing), then return to Chaster setup.

### Which Lockbox is used if I own more than one?

Chaster setup has no box picker. Run an empty-box test to identify which box receives the session before storing keys. Choosing a Chaster lock in Pull does not select a physical box.

### Can I unpair my Lockbox during a lock?

No. Unpairing is blocked while the box holds an active session, including an offer or a break. Dashboard shows *This Lockbox has an active lock session. End or abandon the session before you unpair it.* End or abandon the session first.

### Why can I not create another Chaster lock after unlocking?

An unlocked lock may still use a lock slot. If you reach Chaster's limit, review and archive a completed lock you no longer need. Archiving is separate from physical unlocking.

### Can I disconnect Chaster during a session?

Finish the session first. **Disconnect** is blocked while the linked session is active, on a break, or waiting for device confirmation. It clears stored authorization and retains history; it does not unlock the box or end the Chaster lock.

### Can I use the Lockbox pages in Chaster in my language?

Yes. The **Research And Desire Lockbox** pages inside Chaster follow your browser's language: English, German, Dutch, or French. Switching language keeps the page connected to your lock.

### How do I get my Chaster Plus coupon?

Every Lockbox purchase includes a coupon for 2 months of Chaster Plus. The code is emailed to you within 3 days of your purchase. For help applying it, contact Chaster's support. If the code never arrived, contact [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com).

## Connection loss and emergency access

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep an independent way to access your keys">

Do not rely on an outage to release them. Read the [emergency unlock guide](/lockbox/using/emergency-unlock) and [emergency backplate guide](/lockbox/support/emergency-backplate) before a session with keys.

</Callout>

### What if Wi-Fi or Chaster is unavailable?

Restore connectivity, wake the Lockbox, follow its prompts, and check both platforms and the drawer. Dashboard may show a session before the device catches up.

### Can I turn off software emergency unlock for a Chaster lock?

Yes, before the lock starts:

- **Lock created on Chaster:** turn off emergency unlock in the lock's settings on Chaster. The Lockbox menu then has no **Emergency Unlock** for that session.
- **Lock started from Dashboard:** turn off **Emergency unlock** in the template. The Chaster lock gets the same setting.

A change during a running lock is not sent automatically; **Pull from Chaster** can apply Chaster's current setting. Dashboard's lock settings say *Emergency unlock is disabled for this session* when it is off. The [hardware emergency unlock](/lockbox/support/emergency-backplate) always remains available.

### What happens to Chaster after a software emergency unlock?

Once the server receives the device event, it marks the Dashboard session abandoned and attempts to archive the Chaster lock. Check Chaster afterward: failed sync can leave it running. A hardware emergency opening is not reported automatically.

For 24 hours afterward, no new lock can start on the box. A Chaster lock created during that time stays on Chaster and does not reach the Lockbox.

### What if I use Chaster's own emergency unlock?

The R+D session ends, and the Lockbox opens once it receives the change. The Lockbox's 24-hour cooldown does not apply.

## Optional account permissions

### Do I need to connect my accounts every time?

No. Authorization refreshes automatically when possible. Use **Connect Chaster** again if it cannot refresh or access has been revoked.

### What access does Connect Chaster request?

Profile, locks, shared locks, keyholder features, and messaging. Review the full permission screen before selecting **Allow**.
