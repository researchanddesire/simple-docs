Chastity Lockbox stores a physical key while a local timer, a self-lock session, or a trusted Keyholder manages access.

<Callout type="warn" title="Read safety before locking">

Review [Lockbox Safety and Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety), keep emergency instructions available offline, and complete a short test lock before any extended session.

</Callout>

## Recommended first-use order

1. [Read Safety and Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety).
2. [Pair with Bluetooth](/lockbox/quick-start/pairing). Automatic pairing also provisions Wi-Fi.
3. Choose a self-lock or review the [Keyholder Guide](/lockbox/quick-start/keyholder-guide).
4. Practice software and hardware emergency access while the device is unlocked.
5. [Run a short test lock](/lockbox/quick-start/start-a-lock).
