## Need your key now?

<Cards>
  <Card
    title="Software emergency unlock"
    href="/lockbox/using/emergency-unlock"
  >
    On the locked device, hold **Lock/Enter** for about 2 seconds, then select
    **Emergency Unlock** and confirm. This ends the session and starts a 24-hour
    cooldown. Open the full guide for steps and troubleshooting.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Hardware emergency unlock"
    href="/lockbox/support/emergency-backplate"
  >
    If software unlock is disabled or the device is unresponsive or out of
    power, follow the backplate guide to retrieve your key. This procedure is
    destructive and irreversible; the backplate must be replaced before it can
    lock securely again.
  </Card>
</Cards>

Software Emergency Unlock works locally without internet, when enabled. Losing Wi-Fi does not by itself make the software option unavailable.

## Before storing keys

- **Test with an empty box.** Confirm that the intended drawer physically locks and opens. For Chaster, follow the [empty-box test](/lockbox/chaster/setup#4-start-the-empty-box-test).
- **Prepare independent emergency access.** Read both unlock guides, find the device menu, and make sure you can access the tools required for the hardware method.
- **Check emergency settings before locking.** Software Emergency Unlock can be disabled for a session. Know whether it will be available to you.
- **Check the physical box.** A connected status, ready message, or running timer does not prove the drawer has locked or opened. See [Chaster integration limitations](/lockbox/chaster/limitations).

## After emergency access

Check that the drawer opens and retrieve your key. A hardware opening is not reported automatically, so software may still show a locked session. With Chaster, check both platforms afterward: session cleanup can fail. See the [recovery FAQ](/lockbox/chaster/faq#connection-loss-and-emergency-access).

For a replacement backplate or help resolving session state, contact [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com).
