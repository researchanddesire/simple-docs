---
title: "Check if your Chastity Lockbox is online or offline"
---

Knowing whether your Chastity Lockbox is online ensures you can send settings and receive updates reliably. This guide shows two quick ways to confirm status: from the dashboard and directly on the device.


<Callout type="info">

Before you start: make sure your device is paired to your account and powered on. If you haven’t paired yet, see <a href="/lockbox/quick-start/pairing">Step 2: Pair your Lockbox to your account</a>.

</Callout>
## Check the dashboard indicator

Use the dashboard to confirm your device is connected to your account and currently online.

### Step 1: Confirm the device is paired

Sign in to the dashboard and go to <strong>Settings → Devices</strong>. Verify your Lockbox appears as paired.

<Callout type="success">

You should see your Lockbox listed with its ID.

</Callout>
### Step 2: Open the Chastity Lockbox home page

Navigate to the Lockbox home in the dashboard.

### Step 3: Find the online/offline indicator

Look for the connection indicator in the bottom-left corner of the page. When your device is online, the dashboard will show "Online"; when not connected, it will show "Offline".

<Callout type="info">

The dashboard status may take up to 60 seconds to update after your device connects. No page refresh is required—it syncs automatically.

</Callout>
## Check the device display (real-time)

Your device display shows real-time connectivity and is the most accurate source of truth.

### Step 4: Wake the device

Power on the Lockbox or wake it from sleep.

### Step 5: Wait for Wi‑Fi

When Wi‑Fi connects, the Wi‑Fi icon appears.

### Step 6: Read the status indicator

Look for the small round status indicator next to the Wi‑Fi icon (usually to its left) on the device display.

<Callout type="success">

A solid green dot next to the Wi‑Fi icon means the device is online and connected.

</Callout>
### Status indicator meanings

- Flashing yellow dot: connecting to the dashboard
- Solid green dot: online and connected
- Solid red dot: connected to Wi-Fi but no internet access (check your network)
- No dot visible: offline, or the device is asleep (wake it to check again)

<Callout type="idea">

Trust the device display over the dashboard indicator. The device shows live status; the dashboard can lag by up to a minute.

</Callout>
<Callout type="info">

About sleep modes: in <a href="/lockbox/device-states/sleep-mode">Automatic Sleep</a> the device may dim the screen but typically stays connected; in <strong>Deep Sleep</strong> Wi‑Fi is turned off until you wake the device.

</Callout>
## Troubleshooting

If your device shows offline status or the dashboard says "Offline":

- Make sure the device is awake and the Wi‑Fi icon appears
- Move the device closer to your router or try a different network
- Reconnect Wi‑Fi from the device menu (see Quick Start Step 1)
- Wait up to 60 seconds for the dashboard to sync
- Restart the device if needed
<Callout type="info" title="Reconnect Wi‑Fi">

Go to the device menu and run Wi‑Fi setup again. If you still can’t connect, see <a href="/lockbox/quick-start/wifi-setup">Wi‑Fi Setup</a>.

</Callout>
<Callout type="info" title="The Wi‑Fi icon never appears">

- Verify your network credentials
- Try a 2.4 GHz network if available
- Power cycle your router
- Restart the Lockbox (see <a href="/lockbox/device-states/restarting">Restarting Device</a>)

</Callout>
<Callout type="info" title="The device might be asleep">

If you enabled Deep Sleep, Wi‑Fi is off until you wake the device. See <a href="/lockbox/device-states/sleep-mode">Sleep Mode</a>.

</Callout>
<Callout type="info" title="Still offline after restart">

If the device remains offline after a restart and Wi‑Fi reconnection, check for Wi‑Fi related errors (e.g., E‑Lock 10) under <a href="/lockbox/errors/e-lock-10">Device Errors</a>, or contact support.

</Callout>
<Callout type="info" title="Red dot next to Wi-Fi icon (no internet)">

A red status indicator means your device is connected to Wi-Fi but cannot reach the internet. This is typically a network issue, not a device problem.

**Try these steps:**
1. Verify other devices on the same network can access the internet
2. Restart your router and wait 2 minutes before reconnecting
3. Try connecting to a different Wi-Fi network (such as a mobile hotspot)
4. If using hotel, corporate, or public Wi-Fi, check if you need to complete a captive portal login

If you see a red dot on multiple different networks, <a href="mailto:support@researchanddesire.com">contact support</a> for further troubleshooting.



</Callout>
