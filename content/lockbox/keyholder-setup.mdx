# Add a keyholder

1. Pair the Lockbox with your Dashboard account.
2. Go to **Settings → Partners** in the [R+D Dashboard](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com/).
3. Invite your keyholder and choose the Lockbox access you want to share.
4. Ask them to open the invitation and accept it.
5. Confirm the Lockbox appears for the keyholder before starting a session.

# Agree before locking

Discuss the session length, breaks, emergency release, communication, and circumstances that end the session. Both people should know the backup release plan.

Only give keyholder access to someone you trust. Review or remove access from **Settings → Partners** when it is no longer needed.
