This is the recommended first-use journey for a new Lockbox.

## Before you begin

- Read [Lockbox Safety and Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety).
- Charge the Lockbox for at least 30 minutes.
- Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop for automatic Bluetooth pairing.
- Have the name and password for a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network available.
- Decide whether you are self-locking or inviting a Keyholder.

## 1. Pair with Bluetooth

Follow [Pair Your Lockbox](/lockbox/quick-start/pairing). The automatic flow pairs the device and provisions Wi-Fi in one process.

<Callout type="info">

Use [manual Wi-Fi setup](/lockbox/quick-start/wifi-setup) only when automatic Bluetooth pairing is unavailable or support asks you to use it.

</Callout>

## 3. Practice emergency access

While the Lockbox is unlocked, locate the device menu and review [Software Emergency Unlock](/lockbox/device-states/emergency-unlock). Make sure you can also reach the [hardware backplate instructions](/lockbox/support/emergency-backplate) without relying on the dashboard.

## 4. Run a short test lock

Use [Start Your First Lock](/lockbox/quick-start/start-a-lock). Keep the key accessible in the Lockbox, choose a short duration, verify the expected unlock path, and confirm that the dashboard and device show the same state.

## 5. Confirm success

Your setup is complete when:

- the Lockbox appears in **My Devices**;
- the device shows Wi-Fi and dashboard connectivity;
- the test lock starts and ends as expected; and
- both roles know how to request a break, end the session, and use emergency access.

If any of these checks fail, start with the [Support Center](/lockbox/support).
