title: "How to Start a Lock from the Dashboard"
---

In this guide you'll find direction on how:

1. To start a lock
2. To create a lock template; and
3. Lock conditions change for self-locks versus keyholder locks

<Callout type="info" title="Before starting a lock, ensure:">

1. You have a lockbox paired to your account
2. Your lockbox is online and connected to Wi-Fi
3. You have at least one lock template created

</Callout>

## Starting a 24-hour Lock Session

Navigate to the Chastity Lockbox homepage

<Steps>
  <Step>

### Navigate to the Chastity Lockbox homepage

Run the install command.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Click Start "24-hour lock session"

Set your environment variables.

  </Step>
</Steps>


<Callout type="warn">

You will not be able to abandon nor modify this lock for the 24-hour period.

</Callout>

## Creating a Lock Template

<Callout type="info">

Lock templates allow you or your partner to create and initiate locks with conditions more complex than the simpler 24-hour lock.

</Callout>


### Step 3: Navigate to Templates

From the dashboard home page, under the "Chastity Lockbox" section, click on **Templates**, where you'll see your saved lock configurations.

### Step 4: Select your template

Find the template you want to use for this lock session, or create a custom one. Each template contains your predefined lock settings.

<Callout type="info">

Need to create a template first? Templates can be created in the dashboard under **Chastity Lockbox > Templates**.

</Callout>
### Step 5: Choose your lock mode

Click the **Lock** button on the right side of your chosen template. You'll have two options:

- **No keyholder** - Start a self-lock where you maintain control
- **Select a keyholder** - Choose from your available partners to hold the key

<Callout type="info">

Self-locking is a great option for testing your settings without consequences.

</Callout>
### Step 6: Confirm and start

Click **Confirm** to start the lock. Your device will automatically receive the settings.

<Callout type="success">

Your lock is now active. Test the device to verify it's locked before proceeding with your session.

</Callout>
