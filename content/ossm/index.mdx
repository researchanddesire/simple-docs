The OSSM (Open Source Sex Machine) is the kind of product that does what it says on the tin - it's a sex machine with open source hardware and software for all interested parties to contribute to. The OSSM is a true all-rounder - it is lightweight and portable thanks to its collapsibility, but strong enough to get the job done.

<img src="/ossm/img/ready-to-play-unannotated.webp" alt="OSSM ready to play" />

But enough about that! You've probably just received your OSSM and are excited to put it to good use. Let's get to unboxing if you've received a Ready-to-Play, and building if you've received an OSSM DIY kit.
