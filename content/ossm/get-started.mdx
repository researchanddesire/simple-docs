# Before you start

Place the OSSM on a stable surface with clear space around the moving parts. Make sure no person or object is in its path.

Use the official **24 V, 4 A** power supply. A third-party supply with the wrong voltage, polarity, or current rating can damage the OSSM.

## Power on and home

1. Connect the power cable to the wall outlet.
2. Connect the barrel plug to the OSSM.
3. Watch the status light and allow the OSSM to complete its homing movement.
4. Do not touch the carriage while it is moving.

Homing lets the OSSM measure both ends of its rail. It homes when power is connected and may home again when you select **Simple Penetration** or **Stroke Engine**. The slow back-and-forth movement is expected; keep the rail clear until it stops.

## Choose a controller

- Connect the official wired remote using its Ethernet cable.
- Or follow [Get Started with RADR](/radr/get-started).

The OSSM should finish homing before you begin a session. If it does not, disconnect power and see [Troubleshooting](/ossm/troubleshooting).

Building an OSSM from parts? Use the [official OSSM assembly guide](https://ohai.researchanddesire.com/ossm).
