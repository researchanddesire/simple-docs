## Cleaning Your OSSM

To wipe down any dirty areas we recommend using a microfiber or other gentle cloth dipped in a solution of mild dishsoap and warm water. This will help to remove lube and other residue that may accumulate on the machine during play.

<Callout type="warn" title="Make sure to unplug your OSSM from power before cleaning the machine.">
</Callout>

## Greasing the Linear Rail

Our linear rails come pre-greased, but you may notice a build-up of debris in the rail's inner channel over time. This can cause the rail to move less smoothly. Follow the steps below to re-grease your linear rail:

<Callout type="info" title="Tools you'll need: a microfiber cloth and any food-grade grease.">
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Make sure to unplug your OSSM from power before greasing the linear rail.">
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>

### Manually extend the rail

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Get your grease

Place a small dollop of grease on the rail using a clean finger.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Grease the rail

Using a clean hand, run the grease through the inner channel of the rail.

![linear rail inner channel annotated](./images/linear-rail-inner-channel-annotated-1.webp)

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Clean up

Remove any excess grease using a microfiber cloth. If the grease is stubborn, dip the cloth in a solution of warm water and dish soap and clean again.

  </Step>
</Steps>



<Callout type="success" title="Your rail is now greased!">
</Callout>
