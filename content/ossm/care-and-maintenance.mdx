Disconnect the wall supply and USB before cleaning, inspection, lubrication, or attachment changes. A paused controller is not a safe maintenance state.

## Maintenance schedule

| When                                           | Check or action                                                                                | Stop-use threshold                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Before every session                           | Frame stability, fasteners, belt, rail, attachment, power cable, controller, and Stop action   | Loose, cracked, frayed, bent, contaminated, unusually noisy, or unreliable parts |
| After every session                            | Remove the attachment and wipe residue from accessible surfaces                                | Liquid inside an enclosure or connector                                          |
| Monthly when used regularly                    | Inspect the full belt path, pulley alignment, rail channel, cable strain relief, and enclosure | Belt teeth or edges damaged; rail catches; pulley or fastener moves by hand      |
| When motion becomes rough or debris is visible | Clean and lightly lubricate the linear rail                                                    | Scoring, corrosion, persistent binding, or roughness after cleaning              |
| After transport, a collision, or a repair      | Repeat the unloaded homing, minimum-motion, and Stop tests                                     | Any unexpected motion or failed Stop test                                        |

These are conservative inspection intervals, not a substitute for checking before every use. Inspect more often after heavy use or exposure to dust, lubricant, or transport.

## Clean OSSM

1. Disconnect all power and remove the attachment.
2. Use a microfiber or similarly non-abrasive cloth dampened with warm water and a small amount of mild dish soap.
3. Wipe residue from external surfaces without allowing liquid into the motor, PCB enclosure, connectors, belt, or rail bearings.
4. Wipe again with a clean, lightly damp cloth and dry completely before reconnecting power.

Do not immerse OSSM, spray cleaner directly onto it, or use solvents, abrasives, or high-pressure air.

## Clean and lubricate the rail

Use the lubricant supplied with the machine. If none is available, use only a food-grade grease that its manufacturer explicitly marks compatible with linear bearings and nearby plastics. Contact support before substituting a spray lubricant or a product with unknown material compatibility.

<Steps>
  <Step>

### Disconnect power and expose the rail

Move the carriage by hand only after all power is disconnected. Stop if it binds; do not force it.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Remove debris

Wipe the accessible channel with a clean microfiber cloth. Do not push debris into a bearing or enclosure.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Apply a thin film

Use a clean applicator to place a small amount in the inner rail channel, then move the carriage slowly by hand to distribute it.

![The inner channel of the OSSM linear rail where a thin lubricant film is applied](./images/linear-rail-inner-channel-annotated-1.webp)

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Remove excess and test unloaded

Wipe away excess, reconnect power with the travel path empty, allow homing, and test minimum motion and Stop.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Repair or replace

Do not continue using a part that is cracked, bent, frayed, scorched, corroded, loose after correct tightening, electrically damaged, or unable to move smoothly. Do not operate after a belt skips teeth, an attachment cannot stay secure, a connector overheats, or Stop is unreliable. Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with photos and the affected part before ordering a substitute.
