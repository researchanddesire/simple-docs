---
title: "Power On and Home OSSM"
description: "Connect the supplied 24 V power unit, keep clear of startup movement, and confirm OSSM homes successfully."
---

**Outcome:** OSSM has completed homing and is waiting with motion controls at zero. Allow about two minutes.

## Before you connect power

- Read [OSSM safety and emergency stop](/ossm/safety).
- Stabilise the frame and clear the full rail travel path.
- Set controller speed and depth to zero.
- Use the supplied 24 V, 4 A DC power supply. Do not substitute an unverified supply.

<Steps>
  <Step>

### Connect the power supply to the wall

![The supplied OSSM power supply with its wall lead and DC output identified](./ossm/img/power-supply-annotated.webp)

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Connect the barrel plug to OSSM

![The OSSM power input identified on the enclosed control board](./ossm/img/enclosed-ossm-pcb-annotated1.webp)

Keep hands and objects outside the rail path before making this connection.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Observe homing from a safe distance

![The OSSM control board status LED illuminated during startup](./images/enclosed-ossm-pcb-annotated2.webp)

The rail moves slowly to measure its endpoints. Do not restrain or touch it.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Success">

Homing finishes without obstruction or unusual noise, the status LED remains on, and the carriage is stationary. Continue to [choose and test a controller](/ossm/controllers).

</Callout>

## If homing does not finish

Disconnect power. Check for an obstruction, loose belt, damaged cable, unstable frame, or attachment in the travel path. Do not repeatedly power-cycle a jammed or noisy machine; use [OSSM troubleshooting](/ossm/troubleshooting) or contact support.
