Start here if your OSSM shows an error, won't finish homing, or isn't behaving the way you expect.

<Callout type="warning" title="Make it safe first">

To stop the machine, press and hold the right knob on the Wired Remote for about a second. The machine stops and the remote goes back to the menu.

You can't stop homing with the knob. That includes the homing that runs when you start a mode after a stop, while the screen still shows the menu. If something is wrong while the OSSM homes, if holding the knob doesn't stop it, or if you're not sure, unplug the power supply.

</Callout>

## Find your firmware version

Your OSSM shows its firmware version at the bottom left of the remote screen while it starts up, including on the **Measuring Stroke** screen. Note it down: support will ask for it.

## Still stuck?

Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with:

- your firmware version
- a photo of the label on your power supply
- a short video of the problem. For homing or noise problems, start recording when you plug in the power and keep the rail and the remote screen in view, with sound on.

You can also ask the community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/DHTuY2AZYJ).
