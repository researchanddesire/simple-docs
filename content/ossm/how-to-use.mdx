Use one focused task at a time. If this is your first session, follow the cards from left to right.

<Cards>
  <Card title="1. Power on and home" href="/ossm/power-and-homing">
    Connect the supplied power unit and confirm the startup movement completes
    safely.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Choose a controller" href="/ossm/controllers">
    Connect the wired remote, RADR wireless remote, or web controller and test
    Stop.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Connect Wi-Fi" href="/ossm/connect-wifi">
    Use the web controller or RADR setup portal for a 2.4 GHz network.
  </Card>
  <Card title="4. Operate OSSM" href="/ossm/operation">
    Run the pre-use check, begin at zero, and learn speed, depth, patterns, and
    sensation.
  </Card>
</Cards>

Keep [OSSM safety and emergency stop](/ossm/safety) available throughout the session. For a fault, go to [OSSM troubleshooting](/ossm/troubleshooting).
