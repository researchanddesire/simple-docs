# Before every session

- Put the OSSM on a stable surface and keep its motion path clear.
- Check that fasteners, mounts, the rail, and the attachment are secure.
- Keep hair, clothing, cords, fingers, and other body parts away from pinch points.
- Test the stop control before use and keep it within immediate reach.
- Begin with low speed and short depth.
- Make sure everyone involved can stop the session at any time.

# During use

Stay with the OSSM while it is moving. Stop immediately if there is pain, numbness, an unexpected sound, loose hardware, unstable movement, or loss of control.

# Before adjusting hardware

Stop motion, unplug the power supply, and wait for all parts to stop before changing an attachment, cleaning, lubricating, or inspecting the machine.

Do not use the OSSM if the power cable or supply is damaged.
