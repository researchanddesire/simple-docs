# Clean the OSSM

1. Stop motion and unplug the power supply.
2. Wipe accessible surfaces with a microfiber or other gentle cloth.
3. For ordinary dirt, use a small amount of mild dish soap and warm water on the cloth.
4. Keep liquid away from electronics, connectors, and openings.
5. Let every surface dry before reconnecting power.

Do not spray cleaner directly onto the OSSM.

# Care for the rail

The rail is lubricated before delivery. If debris builds up or movement becomes less smooth:

1. Unplug the OSSM and manually extend the rail.
2. Put a small dollop of food-grade grease on a clean finger.
3. Run the grease through the rail's inner channel.
4. Wipe away excess with a microfiber cloth.

Inspect the power cable, mounts, fasteners, and moving parts regularly. Stop using the OSSM if anything is damaged or loose.
