---
title: "Troubleshoot OSSM"
description: "Diagnose OSSM power, homing, controller, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and unexpected-motion problems safely."
---

Stop and disconnect power before inspecting hardware. Never reach into the travel path while OSSM is powered.

| Symptom                              | First safe action                                   | Next check                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No power or status light             | Disconnect power                                    | Reseat the supplied supply and inspect the cable and connector for damage                 |
| Homing does not finish               | Disconnect power                                    | Clear obstructions and inspect belt, rail, frame stability, and cables                    |
| Controller cannot find OSSM          | Keep motion values at zero                          | Restart only the controller, move closer, and confirm another controller is not connected |
| Controller disconnects during motion | Use Stop; disconnect power if Stop does not respond | Do not resume until an unloaded Stop test passes                                          |
| Wi-Fi will not connect               | Local control may remain available                  | Confirm a 2.4 GHz network, password, range, and internet service                          |
| Wi-Fi indicator is yellow            | Do not repeat setup immediately                     | Wait 60 seconds; the router is connected but the online service is unavailable            |
| Unexpected, rough, or noisy motion   | Stop and disconnect power                           | Inspect for looseness, contamination, obstruction, or damage                              |

## Recovery sequence

1. Record what was on screen and which controller was connected.
2. Stop and disconnect power.
3. Correct only an obvious, non-damaging issue such as a loose external plug or obstruction.
4. Power on with the motion path empty.
5. Confirm homing, minimum unloaded motion, and Stop.

Do not continue after electrical smell, heat, damaged insulation, repeated homing failure, unreliable Stop, belt or rail damage, or unexplained motion. Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with the device version, controller type, status indicators, and the steps already tried.
