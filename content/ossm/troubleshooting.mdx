# No power or status light

1. Disconnect the barrel plug.
2. Confirm the official power supply is connected firmly to a working outlet.
3. Check the cable and plug for visible damage.
4. Reconnect the barrel plug.

Do not use a damaged cable or an unverified power supply.

# Homing does not finish

Disconnect power. Make sure the motion path is clear and nothing is pressing against the carriage. Reconnect power without touching the moving parts. If homing fails again, disconnect power and contact support.

# A controller does not connect

- Let the OSSM finish homing first.
- Confirm the remote cable is fully seated, or restart RADR nearby.
- Disconnect other controllers or browser sessions. Only one controller or app should be connected at a time.
- Restart the OSSM and controller.

Still stuck? Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with the controller type and what the status light or display shows.
