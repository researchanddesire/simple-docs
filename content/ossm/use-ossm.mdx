# Use the wired remote

1. Complete [power-on and homing](/ossm/get-started).
2. Connect the remote to the OSSM with its Ethernet cable.
3. Use the right knob to choose a mode and adjust depth or the selected setting.
4. Turn the left knob slowly to increase speed.

Press the right knob to select or go back. Press and hold it to stop motion.

In **Stroke Engine**, double-click the right knob to open pattern selection.

# Use RADR

After RADR connects to the OSSM:

- Turn the left knob to change speed.
- Turn the right knob to change the active setting, such as depth.
- Press the middle button to pause or stop.
- Use the menu controls to choose a mode.

Use the left and right bumpers to move between **Depth**, **Sensation**, and **Stroke**. The screen shows which setting the right knob currently changes.

<img src="./images/wireless-remote-annotated.webp" alt="RADR wireless remote with its buttons, bumpers, and knobs labelled" width="480" />

See [RADR Controls](/radr/controls) for the full control layout.

Start at the lowest speed and a short depth. Make one change at a time. Keep the stop control within immediate reach throughout the session.
