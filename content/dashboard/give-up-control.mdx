**Give Up Control** hands control of your devices to your partner. It suits power-exchange play: while it's on, your partner decides, and you can't change your own sessions.

<Callout type="warning" title="Agree on a way out first">

Before you turn it on, connect with your partner (see [Partners and keyholders](/dashboard/partners)) and agree on how you'll stop if something goes wrong. If you use a Lockbox, read [Safety & Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety) first.

</Callout>

## Turn on Give Up Control

1. Open **Settings → Advanced**. In the newer menu, select **Use old nav** at the bottom of the sidebar first: **Advanced** isn't in the newer menu yet.
2. Under **Display options**, switch on **Give up control**.
3. In **Choose a Consent Policy**, pick how you'll get control back (see below).
4. Select **Confirm**.

This setting only changes **your** account. Your partner can't turn it on or off for you.

## Choose a consent policy

| Policy | How you get control back |
| --- | --- |
| **Instant** | Switch **Give up control** off at any time. |
| **Delayed** | Switch it off, then wait 72 hours. Your first attempt starts the wait. |
| **Permanent** | You can't switch it off yourself. Only R+D support can. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Permanent means permanent for you">

Your partner can't give control back to you, and removing your partner doesn't switch it off. If you choose **Permanent**, the only way back is to contact R+D support.

</Callout>

## While Give Up Control is on

- You can't start a self-lock. You'll see "You've given up control and can't start a self-lock."
- You can't unlock or end your own lock session from the Dashboard before its timer runs out. You'll see "You've given up control and can't unlock this session."
- Some pages, such as editing a lock session, show "You have given up control and cannot access this page. Please ask your partner to change settings on your behalf."
- If your account has a **Danger zone**, you can't reset your history there.
- You can still create and edit your own Deepthroat Trainer templates, but you can't change which template is active or the schedule type.

## Get control back

### Instant

Open **Settings → Advanced** (old menu) and switch **Give up control** off.

### Delayed

1. Open **Settings → Advanced** and switch **Give up control** off. You'll see "You must wait 72 hours to get control back." This starts the wait.
2. If you try again during the wait, you'll see how many hours are left, for example "You must wait 12 hours to get control back." The setting stays on.
3. After 72 hours, switch **Give up control** off again. It turns off straight away.

### Permanent

Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) from the email address on your account. Only the account holder can ask, and we may check in with you before we make the change.

<Callout type="info" title="Locked right now and need out?">

You don't have to wait for Give Up Control to end. If you need your key now, use [Safety & Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety). You can also end the lock by removing your keyholder with **End lock & remove partner** (see [Remove a partner](/dashboard/partners#remove-a-partner)). Then check that your Lockbox has opened.

</Callout>
