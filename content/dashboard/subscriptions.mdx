---
title: "Subscriptions (Ultra)"
description: "Ultra and the other paid plans have ended, and every Dashboard feature is free. What to do about an old subscription or charge."
---

Every Dashboard feature is free for everyone. There are no plans, no checkout and nothing to subscribe to. This includes your partner or keyholder.

You can check this under **Settings → Subscription**. The page says **Everything's free now!**

## Leftover "Ultra" messages

You may still see a few old Ultra messages. You can ignore them:

- **"You've unlocked $10 off your first Ultra subscription payment!"** after you pair a Lockbox or Trainer. There's nothing to buy, so the code isn't needed.
- **Ultra hints and badges**, such as "Ultra users may change the color of the lock that appears on their device". The feature works for everyone.

If a feature still asks you for Ultra or won't work without it, that's a bug. Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with a screenshot and the page you were on.

## Cancel an old subscription

The Dashboard has no billing page, so you can't see or cancel an old subscription there.

If you had Ultra, Pioneer or Founder and you're still being charged, email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com). Include:

- the email address you subscribed with
- the date and amount of each charge
- a screenshot of the statement line, with your card number hidden

Never send us your full card number.

Deleting your Dashboard account doesn't cancel an old subscription. If you had one, ask us to check before you delete your account.
