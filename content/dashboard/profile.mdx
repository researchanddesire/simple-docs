Your login details live on **Settings → My Profile**. In the newer menu, open your profile menu, select **Settings**, then **Profile**. Your username and public profile live on your profile page.

## Change your email address

1. Sign in and open **Settings → My Profile**.
2. Under **Account settings**, select **Change email** next to your email address.
3. Enter your new address in **New Email Address** and select **Send Code**.
4. We send a 6-digit code to your **new** address. Enter it and select **Verify**.
5. When you see "Email address updated successfully!", you're done. Sign in with your new address from now on.

No code? Check spam, then select **Resend code**. If **Resend code** stays greyed out, reload the page and start again from **Change email**.

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Please enter a different email address" | You entered your current address. Enter the new one. |
| "Invalid or expired verification code. Please try again." | Use the code from the newest email, or select **Resend code**. |
| "Unable to update your email address. Please try again." | The new address may already belong to another Dashboard account. Use a different address, or contact support. |

Changing your Dashboard email does not change the email on your store orders. Your past orders keep the address you used at checkout.

### Lost access to your old email?

If you can still sign in with your password or Discord, change your email yourself using the steps above.

If you can't sign in and no longer have access to the old mailbox, email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com). Tell us your username and the new address you'd like to use.

## Change your password

1. Open **Settings → My Profile**.
2. Under **Account password**, select **Change password**.
3. Enter your **Current Password**, a **New Password** and the same password again in **Confirm New Password**, then select **Update password**.

Your new password needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character.

<Callout type="info" title="Never set a password?">

If you always sign in with the email link or Discord, you don't have a current password to enter. Instead, open your profile menu and select **Sign out** (**Log out** in the newer menu). Then, on the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?** under the password box and follow [Forgot your password?](/dashboard/sign-in#forgot-your-password) to create one. If the page shows the email-link form, select **Email and password** first.

</Callout>

## Change your username and profile

1. Open your profile menu and select **View profile** (**Profile** in the newer menu).
2. On your profile card, select **Edit profile**.
3. Change your **Username**, and add **Pronouns**, **Orientations**, **Genders**, **Roles** or **About me** if you like.
4. Select **Save**.

Usernames are 4 to 24 characters long and use letters, numbers, underscores (\_) and dashes (-). If you see "Username is already taken.", try another one. Your username is what partners search for when they invite you.

### Profile picture and cover image

Open **Settings → My Profile** and use the **Profile picture** and **Cover image** sections. Images can be up to 5 MB.

Other signed-in Dashboard users can open your profile page by your username. It shows your username, pictures, the profile details and photos you add, your points and league, and an **Activity History** of the days you used a Lockbox or Trainer over the past year. Your partners can also see an active lock there, and so can everyone else while that lock is public.

## Choose which emails you get

1. Open **Settings → Advanced**. In the newer menu, select **Use old nav** at the bottom of the sidebar first: **Advanced** isn't in the newer menu yet.
2. Under **Email settings**, turn **Email notifications** on or off. When it's on, choose the categories you want, such as **Lockbox events**, **Trainer events**, **Sharing invites** and **Leaderboard events**.

Selecting **Unsubscribe** in one of our notification emails turns off **all** Dashboard email notifications and every category. To get them back, turn **Email notifications** on again in **Settings → Advanced**, then turn on each category you want.

<Callout type="warning" title="Keyholders: keep Lockbox events on">

**Lockbox events** emails tell you when a lock starts, when a break is missed, and when an emergency unlock is used. If you turn them off, or unsubscribe from all emails, you won't get these alerts. Your own setting doesn't change what your partner receives.

</Callout>
