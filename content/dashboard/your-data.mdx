This page explains what the R+D Dashboard keeps about you and your devices. For our store and general privacy practices, read our [privacy policy](https://www.researchanddesire.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## Your account

- **Login details:** your email address and how you sign in.
- **Setup choices:** your confirmation that you're 18 or older, and whether you chose **Product Updates** emails.
- **Profile:** your username, and the profile picture, cover image, pronouns, orientations, genders, roles and **About me** text you choose to add.
- **Email settings:** which notification emails you want.

## Your devices and sessions

| Device | What the Dashboard stores |
| --- | --- |
| **Lockbox** | Its hardware address and pairing code, firmware version and when it last connected. Your lock sessions and their timeline: starts, breaks, time changes, unlocks and votes. Activity reports from the box: battery level, charging, and whether the paddle is locked and the backplate is closed. |
| **Deepthroat Trainer** | Its Trainer ID, firmware version and when it last connected. Your training sessions, segments and points, your templates and schedules, and the toys you add. |
| **OSSM** | Its hardware address, firmware version and when it last connected. Your OSSM sessions and per-stroke data. |

## Saved Wi-Fi networks

If you save a Wi-Fi network in the Dashboard, we store the network name and password. The password is stored encrypted. It's used to send Wi-Fi details to your devices over Bluetooth during setup.

## Partners

We store who you're connected to and any invitations you've sent or received. Partners you've given access to can see and change your devices' sessions and settings while they're connected.

## Notifications

We keep a record of the notifications we send you, in the Dashboard, by email, by browser push or to Discord, including what they said and whether they were delivered. If you turn on browser push notifications or add a Discord webhook, we store those details so we can deliver them.

## How you use the Dashboard

We record how the Dashboard is used, to find bugs and improve it. This can include recordings of Dashboard sessions. In those recordings, everything you type into a form is masked. We don't record the developer and app-connection pages.

## Help requests

When you send a message from the Dashboard's help form, it goes to our support team together with your account email address. An automated tool writes a subject line for the request and sets its priority.

If you need your Lockbox key urgently, don't wait for a reply: use [Safety & Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety).

## Who can see what

- **Other signed-in Dashboard users** can open your profile page by your username. They see your username, pictures, the profile details and photos you add, your points and league, and an **Activity History** of the days you used a Lockbox or Trainer over the past year. While a lock is public, they can see that lock there too. Leaderboards show your username, profile picture and points.
- **Partners** see the devices, sessions and settings of accounts that gave them access. Any partner, whichever way access goes, can also see your active lock on your profile page.
- **Discreet mode** (Settings → Advanced) blurs images and anonymises user details on **your** screen only. It doesn't change what others see.

## Change or remove your data

- Change your email, password, username or profile: [Change your email, password or username](/dashboard/profile).
- Stop notification emails: [Choose which emails you get](/dashboard/profile#choose-which-emails-you-get).
- Clear your session history but keep your account: [Start fresh instead](/dashboard/delete-account#start-fresh-instead).
- Delete your account: [Delete your account](/dashboard/delete-account).

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com). We may need to confirm it's you first.
