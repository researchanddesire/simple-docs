You can't delete your account from inside the Dashboard. Our support team deletes it for you when you ask.

<Callout type="warning" title="Deleting your account can't be undone">

If you might come back, consider [starting fresh](#start-fresh-instead) instead.

</Callout>

## Before you ask

1. **End your lock sessions.** Make sure you're not locked, and that no one is locked with you as their keyholder. If you hold the key for someone, end or hand over their lock first.
2. **Unpair your devices.** Open **My Devices** and select **Unpair** for each device. Deleting your account doesn't unpair them, so nobody could pair them again until we step in. See [Unpair a device](/dashboard/devices#unpair-a-device).
3. **Had Ultra, Pioneer or Founder?** Tell us, so we can check that no old subscription is still charging you. See [Subscriptions](/dashboard/subscriptions#cancel-an-old-subscription).

## Ask us to delete your account

Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) **from the email address on your Dashboard account**, and ask us to delete your Dashboard account. We may need to confirm it's you before we delete anything.

When your account is deleted, your partner connections end. Your partners lose access to your devices, and you lose access to theirs.

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

These points are policy, not code, so they're not stated above. Decide what we publish:

- **What deletion removes.** Today the admin deletion removes partner connections and the login, and marks the account deleted. It does **not** remove lock, training or OSSM history, saved Wi-Fi networks, profile details, store orders, email-marketing contacts, analytics or support history.
- **Full erasure.** Whether "delete my data" also covers the store, email-marketing, analytics and support systems, and who handles it.
- **Timing.** How fast we act (current practice: same day to 3 days) and the legal deadline we commit to.
- **Signing up again.** An email address from a deleted account may not be able to register again (known issue RAD-2104). Say so, or fix it first.
- **Copy of your data.** Whether and how we provide one on request.

</Callout>

## Start fresh instead

If you want to keep your account but clear your history, you may be able to do it yourself:

1. Open **Settings → Advanced**. In the newer menu, select **Use old nav** at the bottom of the sidebar first.
2. Under **Danger zone**, find **Reset account data**.
3. Choose what to wipe: **Deepthroat Trainer (DTT)**, **Lockbox (LKBX)** or **Open Source Sex Machine (OSSM)**.
4. Select **Reset selected data**.
5. In **Are you absolutely sure?**, type **RESET MY DATA** under **Type to confirm**, then select **Reset data**.

This keeps your account, paired devices, templates and partners. It can't be undone.

| You choose | What's deleted |
| --- | --- |
| **Deepthroat Trainer (DTT)** | All training sessions, segments and points. Your XP, rank and streak are reset. |
| **Lockbox (LKBX)** | All completed lock sessions, segments, votes and Lockbox activity history. |
| **Open Source Sex Machine (OSSM)** | All OSSM sessions and per-stroke data. |

You can't reset your data while [Give Up Control](/dashboard/give-up-control) is on.

If you don't see **Danger zone**, email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) and ask us to reset your history.

</Callout>
