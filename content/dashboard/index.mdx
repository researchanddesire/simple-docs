## Browser requirements

Most of the Dashboard works in any modern browser. Two features need more:

- **Bluetooth features**, such as automatic OSSM pairing, need Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on an Android phone. They do not work on iPhone, Safari or Firefox.
- **The USB web flasher** needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop computer.

## Where things are

The Dashboard menu has a **Settings** group. You'll use these pages most:

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **My Devices** | Pair and unpair your devices |
| **Partners** | Invite partners, accept invitations, remove a partner |
| **My Profile** | Email address, password, profile picture and cover image |
| **Advanced** | Email notifications, Give Up Control, discreet mode |
| **Subscription** | Confirms that every feature is free |

If you switched on the newer menu (**Try new nav**), **My Devices** and **People** sit at the top of the sidebar. **People** is the same as **Partners**. For **My Profile**, open your profile menu, select **Settings**, then **Profile**. **Advanced** isn't in the newer menu yet: select **Use old nav** at the bottom of the sidebar to reach it.
