You need a free Dashboard account to pair devices, run sessions from your phone or computer, and connect with a partner. Your partner or keyholder needs their own account too.

## Create an account

1. Go to [dashboard.researchanddesire.com](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com).
2. Next to **Not registered?**, select **Sign up here!** The page heading changes to **Create your account**.
3. Choose how you want to sign in from now on:
   - **Email and password:** enter your email address and a password, then select **Create account**.
   - **Email link:** select **Email link (no password required)**, enter your email address, then select **Send registration email**. Continue with [Sign in with an email link and code](#sign-in-with-an-email-link-and-code).
   - **Discord:** select **Discord** and approve the request on Discord.

Your password needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character.

If you see **E-mail Confirmation**, open the link in the email we sent you to confirm your address. Check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived.

<Callout type="info" title="Were you invited by a partner?">

If you opened an invitation, the sign-up page starts with "*username* has invited you to play with them!". Create your account from that page with **email and password** or with **Discord**, and the invitation is waiting for you.

If you sign up with the email link instead, the invitation is not added to your new account. Ask your partner to cancel it and send a new one to your **username**. See [Partners and keyholders](/dashboard/partners).

</Callout>

### Set up your account

The first time you sign in, the Dashboard walks you through four short steps. It brings you back to them until you finish.

1. **Consent settings.** Tick **I am 18 years of age or older**. This is required. **Product Updates** emails are optional.
2. **Choose your username.** Type a username or pick a suggestion, then select **Confirm Username**. Other users see your username. Use 4 to 24 characters: letters, numbers, underscores (\_) and dashes (-).
3. **Connect with a user.** Search for a partner by username, or select **Skip for now**.
4. **Pair your devices.** Pair a device now, or select **Skip for now**. If you paired a device, select **Finish**.

## Sign in

Go to [dashboard.researchanddesire.com](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com) and use the method you chose when you signed up.

### Sign in with your password

Enter your email address and password, then select **Sign in**.

### Sign in with an email link and code

1. Select **Email link (no password required)**.
2. Enter your email address and select **Send sign-in email**.
3. The **Sign-in email sent** page opens. Leave it open.
4. Open the email from us. Then do one of these:
   - Type the **6-digit code** from the email into **Code** on the page you left open. You're signed in as soon as the last digit is entered.
   - Or select the link in the email. Open it in the **same browser** you used in step 2.

<Callout type="idea" title="Reading your email on a different device?">

Use the code. A sign-in link only works in the browser that asked for it, and only once. If you asked on your computer and read the email on your phone, type the code on your computer.

</Callout>

No email after a few minutes? Check your spam or junk folder. Then select **Resend email** on the same page. If it stays greyed out, select **Sign in another way** and request a new email from the sign-in page. To use a password or Discord instead, select **Sign in another way**.

### Sign in with Discord

Select **Discord** and approve the request on Discord. If you cancel on Discord, the Dashboard shows **Sign-in was cancelled**. Try again, or use another method.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?** under the password box.
2. Enter your email address and select **Send Reset Link**.
3. We email you a link **and** a code. Then do one of these:
   - Open the link in the same browser, enter a **New Password** and select **Reset Password**.
   - Or select **I have a code** on the page where you asked for the reset. Enter your email, the **Reset Code** and a **New Password**, then select **Reset Password**. Use this if you read the email on a different device.

If the code is rejected, you'll see "Invalid or expired code. Please try again." Select **Back to email**, then **Resend Reset Link**, and use the code from the newest email.

## "Welcome back!" and a request for a new password

If your account was created on our older system, signing in with your password sends you an email instead and shows **Welcome back!** with "Thanks for coming back! We've updated our authentication system…".

1. Open the newest email from us. Type its code on the **Welcome back!** page, or open its link in the same browser.
2. Set a new password when asked, and select **Reset Password**.

Until you set a new password, every Dashboard page takes you back to the password screen. After that you can sign in normally.

## Fix sign-in problems

| You see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This sign-in link has expired** | Sign-in links work once, for a limited time. | Request a new email and type the code instead. |
| **This sign-in link is no longer valid** | The link was already used, was opened in a different browser, or an email app or security scanner opened it first. | Request a new email and type the code instead. |
| **Sign-in was cancelled** | You declined the request on Discord. | Try again, or use another sign-in method. |
| "The email or password is incorrect." | The address or password doesn't match an account. | Check the address. Use **Forgot password?**, or sign in with the email link. |
| "An account with this email already exists…" | You already have an account with this address. | Select **Sign in** instead, or use **Forgot password?**. |
| "Too many attempts. Please wait a moment and try again." | Too many tries in a short time. | Wait a few minutes, then try once. |
| The **E-mail Confirmation** page, after you sign in with your password | Your address isn't confirmed yet. We've sent the confirmation email again. | Open the confirmation email. Check spam. |
| "This password is known to be weak…" | The password is too easy to guess, or has appeared in a known data leak. | Choose a different password. |
| "This page is out of date. Reload it and try again." | The Dashboard was updated while the page was open. | Select **Reload page**. |

Still stuck? Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) from the address on your account. Tell us which sign-in method you used, the exact message, and your browser and device.
