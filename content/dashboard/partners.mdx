---
title: "Partners and Keyholders"
description: "Share control of your devices with a partner or keyholder: invite them, accept an invitation, switch between accounts, and remove a partner safely."
---

A partner connection links two Dashboard accounts so one person can control the other person's devices. It works for the Lockbox, Deepthroat Trainer and OSSM. Each person keeps their own account and their own login.

Partner accounts are free, so your keyholder doesn't need to buy anything. They need their own account at [dashboard.researchanddesire.com](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com). See [Create an account and sign in](/dashboard/sign-in).

<Callout type="warning" title="Only share control with someone you trust">

A partner can start, change and end sessions on your devices. Before you start, agree on limits, how either of you can stop, and what happens if a device goes offline. Read [Safety & Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety) if you use a Lockbox.

</Callout>

## Who controls whom

When you send an invitation, you choose the direction:

| You choose | After your partner accepts | Your Partners page shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Give them access to my devices** | Your partner controls your devices. Use this if you wear the Lockbox or use the Trainer. | "This user can control and modify your devices" |
| **Request access to their devices** | You control your partner's devices. Use this if you're the keyholder. | "You can control and modify this user's devices" |

If both of you share access with each other, the page shows "You can both control and modify each other's devices".

## Invite a partner

1. Open **Settings → Partners**. In the newer menu, open **People**.
2. Select **Create Invitation**.
3. In **Search for partners by email or username**, type at least 3 characters and select your partner from the list.
4. Under **Access type**, choose **Give them access to my devices** or **Request access to their devices**.
5. Select **Send Invitation**.

Your invitation shows under **Pending Invitations** as "You sent an invitation" until your partner accepts. To withdraw it, select **Cancel** next to it.

### Invite someone who doesn't have an account yet

Type their full email address. You'll see "We'll send an email invitation to this address". They get an email with a **Create Account** button.

They must create their account from that button using **email and password** or **Discord**. If they create it with the email link instead, your invitation won't reach them. In that case, cancel it and send a new one to their **username**.

## Accept an invitation

1. Open **Settings → Partners** (or **People**).
2. Under **Pending Invitations**, check what the invitation asks for:
   - "This user would like to give you access to their devices": you'll control their devices.
   - "This user would like to get access to your devices to modify settings": they'll control yours.
3. Select **Accept**, or **Decline**.

We also email you when someone invites you, if your **Sharing invites** emails are on. The **Review Request** button in that email opens your Dashboard settings. Go to **Settings → Partners** to find the invitation.

## Switch to your partner's account

Once you control a partner's devices, you work on them from your partner's account view.

1. Select the account switcher at the top of the sidebar. It shows **Your Account** when you're on your own account.
2. Under **Users You Control**, select your partner. The account you're viewing is marked **Active**.
3. Use the menu as usual. The device pages now show your partner's devices.
4. To go back, open the switcher again and select **Your Account**.

**Control a new account** at the bottom of the switcher takes you to the Partners page.

In the newer menu (**Try new nav**) there is no account switcher. Open the device switcher at the top of the sidebar and pick your partner's device: it's listed under their username. Your own devices are listed under **My Devices**.

<Callout type="info" title="Check whose account you're on">

Always check the switcher before you start a session. If it shows **Your Account** (or **Owned by you** under the device name in the newer menu), a lock you start goes on **your own** device, not your partner's.

You can't pair a device for your partner. If you see "Your partner doesn't have a…", your partner needs to pair it on their own account first.

</Callout>

## For keyholders: control your partner's Lockbox

1. Switch to your partner's account.
2. In the **Chastity Lockbox** menu, open **Lock Sessions** to see their lock templates.
3. Next to a template, select **Lock** (on a phone, just the padlock icon). Under **Select keyholders**, your username is already ticked. Leave it ticked (selecting it again unticks it), then select **Start lock session**. If the list offers **Self Lock (No Keyholder)**, you're on your own account: close it and switch accounts first.
4. In **Confirm Lock Activation**, check that your username is listed as the keyholder, then select **Confirm**. The Dashboard opens your partner's Lockbox page.
5. If the Lockbox card shows **Awaiting acceptance…**, your partner needs to accept the new lock on the Lockbox itself.
6. While the lock runs, use the Lockbox card:
   - The main button changes with the session. It shows **Break** when a break is allowed, **Resume** during a break, and otherwise **Unlock**, which opens the Lockbox and completes the session.
   - Select the arrow next to it (**More actions**) for **Take a break**, **Add or remove time**, **Modify session**, **End session** and **Abandon session**. **End session** also opens the Lockbox and completes the session.

**Abandon session** can't be undone. It stays on your partner's record as an Abandoned Lock.

For breaks, hidden time and voting, see [Take a Break](/lockbox/using/take-a-break), [Hiding Time](/lockbox/using/hiding-time) and [Public Voting](/lockbox/using/public-voting).

## Remove a partner

Removing a partner ends access in **both** directions.

1. Open **Settings → Partners** (or **People**).
2. Next to your partner, open the **…** menu and select **Remove Partner**.

If you and this partner share an active lock, the Dashboard asks **End current lock with this partner?** You have three choices:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **End lock & remove partner** | The lock ends first, then the partner is removed. If **you** are locked, the lock is recorded as abandoned. If **you** are the keyholder, the lock is ended normally. |
| **Remove Partner** | The partner is removed, but the lock **keeps running**. Your former partner can no longer switch to your account to manage it. |
| **Cancel** | Nothing changes. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Locked and want out?">

If you need your key right now, use [Safety & Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety). Otherwise, if you're the one locked, choose **End lock & remove partner**, not **Remove Partner**. Then check that your Lockbox has opened. If it hasn't, use [Safety & Emergency Access](/lockbox/safety).

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

This page tells a locked person they can end their lock by removing their keyholder. The published Lockbox FAQ says the opposite ("Avoid removing your keyholder while a lock session is active", contact support instead). Confirm we want to document this exit, and update the FAQ to match.

</Callout>

Removing a partner does **not** turn off Give Up Control. See [Give Up Control](/dashboard/give-up-control).

## Fix partner problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The invitation never arrived | Check **Pending Invitations** on **Settings → Partners**. If you were invited by email and signed up with the email link, ask your partner to cancel the invitation and send a new one to your username. |
| "You can't accept this invite if you don't have a trainer." | Open **Settings → Partners** and accept it under **Pending Invitations** instead. |
| "User not found." | Type at least 3 characters and pick your partner from the search results, or enter their full email address. |
| "You cannot invite yourself. But I like your style." | You entered your own username or email. |
| "Error creating user access" | Reload **Settings → Partners**. If your partner is in the list, you're already connected. If not, ask them to send a new invitation. |
| A lock started on the keyholder's own box | The keyholder was on **Your Account**. End or abandon that lock if the Dashboard lets you, or contact support if it doesn't. Then switch to the partner's account and start again. |

Still stuck? Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) from the address on your account, with both usernames. For privacy and consent reasons, we only change access for the account holder who asks.
