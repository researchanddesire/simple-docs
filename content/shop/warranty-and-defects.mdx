## What to send us

If the help pages don't solve it, email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with:

1. Your order number, or the email address you used to order. If you bought it somewhere else or second-hand, tell us where and when.
2. Which product it is, and its firmware version and device ID (see below).
3. A short video or a few photos that show the problem. Please film or photograph the device only.
4. What you've already tried.

### Find your firmware version and device ID

| Product | Firmware version | Device ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OSSM | Bottom-left corner of the start-up screens | Listed as **OSSM MAC Address** under **My Devices** on the dashboard, once paired |
| Chastity Lockbox | Start-up screen, under "Chastity Lockbox" ("Version …") | The code in **Settings > Pairing** on the device (the Lockbox must be on Wi-Fi). The code changes every time the Lockbox restarts. |
| Deepthroat Trainer | Bottom-left of the first screen when you switch it on. The serial number is bottom-right. | **Trainer ID**, shown on the next screen |
| OSSM Wireless Remote | Not shown on the remote. Tell us roughly when you bought it. | We'll ask if we need it |

## What happens next

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We'll help you find the cause first. If a part or device turns out to be faulty, we replace it free of charge.

- Most of our products are built from parts you can swap yourself, so we usually send the part that failed and, where we have one, a guide to fitting it.
- If the whole device is faulty, we send a replacement device.
- You usually don't need to send the faulty item back. Occasionally we ask for it back so we can find out what went wrong. If we do, we send you a prepaid return label.

</Callout>

## Opening, repairing and modifying your device

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Our hardware is open source and designed to be repaired. Opening your device or replacing a part yourself doesn't, on its own, stop us from helping you.

We may not replace parts free of charge when the damage was caused by a modification or repair, a fall or other accident, or the wrong power supply. In those cases we'll still help you get it working, often with replacement parts at a discount.

</Callout>

## Bought second-hand or from another shop?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We'll still help you troubleshoot. Whether we can replace a faulty part for free depends on the case, so tell us where and when the device was bought. If the device is still linked to someone else's account, see [Device ownership and transfers](/shop/device-ownership).

</Callout>

## Replacement parts you can buy

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Many spare parts, such as OSSM boards, motors, harnesses, power supplies and printed parts, and Deepthroat Trainer back boards, are sold in our [store](https://www.researchanddesire.com). For other parts, email us and we'll send you a quote and an invoice. For example:

- **Lockbox replacement back cover:** $10 USD plus shipping.
- **Deepthroat Trainer replacement battery:** about $8 USD plus shipping.
- **OSSM Wireless Remote:** we can send replacement components or a replacement remote.

</Callout>
