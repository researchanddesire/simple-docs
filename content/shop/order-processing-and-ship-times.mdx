This page explains how long your order takes to leave us, and what to expect once it ships.

## How long until my order ships?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Your order joins our queue in Toronto. How long it takes to ship depends on what you ordered and how many orders are ahead of yours.

| What you're ordering | Ships within |
| --- | --- |
| OSSM Ready-to-Play (built to order) | 4 weeks |
| OSSM kits, motors, boards and parts | 3 weeks, usually sooner |
| OSSM Wireless Remote (in stock) | 1 week |
| Chastity Lockbox | 2 weeks |
| Deepthroat Trainer and add-ons | 2 weeks |
| Everything else | 3 weeks |

Checked October 2026 against our shipping records for the previous six months. Pre-order items ship when their batch is ready.

These times are our honest estimate, not a guarantee. If your order includes items from more than one row, it normally ships in one parcel when the last item is ready.

</Callout>

## Why does it take time?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Most of what we sell is assembled, programmed or packed by hand after you order:

- **Ready-to-Play machines** are assembled after you order.
- **OSSM motors** are programmed before they ship.
- **Kits and parts** are counted and packed by hand.
- **Pre-order items** have their own ship dates, which depend on the item.

Busy periods make the queue longer. The biggest one is the Black Friday sale in late November.

</Callout>

## Does faster shipping make it arrive sooner?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Only after it ships. The shipping options at checkout, such as "Express tracked air shipping (5-8 business days)", describe how long the carrier takes **after your parcel leaves us**. They don't shorten the time your order spends in our queue.

</Callout>

## When do I get tracking?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We email you a shipping confirmation with a tracking link when we create your shipping label. The carrier collects parcels from us on set days, so tracking can show only "label created" for up to 3 business days before the first scan.

</Callout>

## If your order is running late

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

If your order is going to ship later than we told you, we'll email you **before that happens** with a new estimate. If you'd rather not wait, reply to cancel and we'll refund you in full to your original payment method.

</Callout>

## Need it by a certain date?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We can't guarantee a delivery date, because carriers and customs can add time after your parcel leaves us. If you need your order for a trip or an event, email us **before you order**. We'll tell you the current estimate for your products. We can also hold an order and ship it on a date you choose.

</Callout>

## Check on an order

Email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with your order number. If your order has already shipped, your shipping confirmation email has the tracking link.
