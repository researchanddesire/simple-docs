When you pair a Chastity Lockbox, Deepthroat Trainer or OSSM, it's linked to your dashboard account. This page explains how to pass a device on, how to set up a device you bought second-hand, and what happens if ownership is disputed.

## How ownership works

- A paired device belongs to **one** dashboard account.
- If you enter the pairing code of a device that's linked to another account, the dashboard shows: "Device already paired, please contact support if you believe this is an error."
- Only the account that owns the device can unpair it from the dashboard.
- The OSSM Wireless Remote isn't linked to a dashboard account, so it doesn't need to be transferred.

## Selling or giving away your device

Unpair the device before you hand it over, so the new owner can set it up without contacting us.

1. **Lockbox only: end any lock session and empty the box first.** If you unpair a Lockbox during a lock, it restarts and restores the lock it has saved.
2. Sign in to the [dashboard](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com) and open **My Devices**.
3. Under **Paired devices**, select **Unpair** next to the device. It's unpaired straight away, with no extra confirmation step.
4. Get the device ready for its new owner:
   - **Lockbox:** it restarts by itself once it's online, and is then ready to pair.
   - **OSSM:** nothing else to do. When the new owner opens **Pairing** in the OSSM menu, the OSSM checks with the dashboard and shows a new pairing code.
   - **Deepthroat Trainer:** the next time it starts with Wi-Fi, it shows "Pair me for more settings and fun!" and its Trainer ID.

If you can't sign in to the account that owns the device, see [Lost access to your account](#lost-access-to-your-account).

## Bought a second-hand device?

If pairing shows "Device already paired, please contact support if you believe this is an error.", the device is still linked to the previous owner's account.

1. Ask the person you got it from to unpair it, using the steps above. This is the quickest way.
2. If they can't, email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with:
   - the device code (see the table below),
   - your proof of purchase, and
   - the email address of the dashboard account you want to use.

### Find the device code

| Device | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Chastity Lockbox | On the Lockbox, open **Settings > Pairing**. The screen shows "Pairing:" followed by a code. The code changes every time the Lockbox restarts, so read it just before you write to us. If the Lockbox is offline, it opens Wi-Fi setup first. Once it's connected, the code appears. |
| Deepthroat Trainer | Switch it on. After the start-up logo, the screen shows **Trainer ID** and a short code. |
| OSSM | Open **Pairing** in the OSSM menu. If the OSSM isn't linked to an account, it shows "Pair OSSM" and a code. If it's still linked to an account, it shows "Paired!" and "Your OSSM is now linked to your account." instead of a code, even if that account isn't yours. Tell us that in your email. If the OSSM isn't on Wi-Fi, it opens Wi-Fi setup first. Once it's connected, the pairing screen appears. |

If you have an OSSM Wireless Remote, it can tell you too. While the remote is connected to the OSSM, select **Pairing** in its menu. "Already Paired" means the OSSM is linked to a dashboard account. If that isn't your account, the OSSM is still linked to someone else.

<Callout type="idea" title={"Tip"}>

Pair a Deepthroat Trainer to your account before you train with it. Training results from a Trainer that isn't paired aren't saved to the dashboard.

</Callout>

## Lost access to your account

If your device is linked to an account you can no longer sign in to, for example because you no longer have that email address, email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with the device code and details that show the device is yours.

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

The quickest way to show it's yours is to email us **from the address you used to order**, or to forward your order confirmation. Once we've confirmed it, we release the device so you can pair it to your current account, usually the same day.

</Callout>

## If ownership is disputed

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Sometimes we're asked to move a device that is still linked to someone else's account, and that person hasn't agreed. This is what we do:

1. **We check your proof of purchase.** We accept an order confirmation from our store or a receipt from another shop (for example Etsy or a reseller).
2. **We contact the original buyer.** If our records show who first bought the device, we email them at the address on their order and ask whether they agree to the transfer.
3. **We decide:**
   - If they agree, we move the device to your account straight away.
   - If they don't reply within **30 days**, we move the device to your account.
   - If they object, we don't move it, and we'll tell you what we can do next.

We never share the other person's name, email address or account details with you, and we never share yours with them.

</Callout>

## If someone has taken over your device

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

If a device you use was moved to another account without your permission, email us straight away. If it's a Lockbox and you need your keys, follow [Lockbox safety and emergency access](/lockbox/safety) first.

</Callout>
