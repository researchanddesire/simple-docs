Use these pages before and after you order. They explain how long your order takes to leave us, how it travels, what import fees to expect, and how to get help if something arrives damaged, goes missing, or stops working.

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Every R+D order ships from our warehouse in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Many products are assembled, flashed or packed by hand after you order, so please read [Order processing and ship times](/shop/order-processing-and-ship-times) before you buy if you need your order by a certain date.

</Callout>
