---
title: "Cancel or Change an Order"
description: "Cancel an order, add or remove items, upgrade shipping or fix your address before your order ships."
---

To cancel or change an order, email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with your order number and what you'd like to change. Ask as early as you can.

## Cancel an order

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

You can cancel any order that hasn't shipped yet. There's no cancellation fee.

- We refund the full amount to your original payment method.
- Your bank or card usually shows the refund within **5–10 business days**. PayPal refunds can take up to 10 business days.
- If part of your order has already shipped, we cancel and refund the items that haven't shipped yet.

If your order is packed and waiting for the carrier, we'll try to take it off the pickup. Tell us as soon as you can.

</Callout>

## Your order has already shipped

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We can't stop a parcel once the carrier has it. If you no longer want it, you can refuse the delivery. When the parcel is back with us, we refund the price of your products. We can't refund the original shipping cost or any fees the carrier charged you.

</Callout>

## Add, remove or swap items

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

You can change an order until it ships.

- **Add an item:** we send you an invoice for the extra item. Once it's paid, the item ships with your order. Adding an item doesn't change your place in the queue.
- **Remove an item:** we remove it and refund the difference, including any shipping difference.
- **Swap a variant** (for example wired to wireless, or a different colour): we invoice or refund the price difference. For bigger changes, such as Ready-to-Play to DIY kit, we may cancel and refund your order so you can place a new one.

</Callout>

## Upgrade your shipping

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Before your order ships, we can move it to a faster shipping option. We'll send you an invoice for the difference. Faster shipping shortens the time in transit only, not the time before your order ships.

</Callout>

## Hold an order or ship it in parts

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

- **Hold:** if you'll be away, we can keep your order and ship it on a date you choose.
- **Ship in parts:** if one item is holding up your order, ask us. We can sometimes ship the ready items first, but you may need to pay for the extra shipping, and we can't always do it during busy periods.

</Callout>

## Change your shipping address

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

- **Before your order ships:** email us the correct address. We'll update it and confirm the change by email.
- **After your order ships:** we can't always redirect a parcel. With some carriers, you can request a change yourself from the carrier's tracking page. Email us and we'll tell you the options for your carrier.
- **If the parcel comes back to us:** see [Damaged, missing or lost items](/shop/damaged-missing-or-lost#returned-to-sender).

</Callout>

## Change your billing address

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We can't change the billing address after you've paid. If you need a different billing address, we can cancel your order with a full refund so you can order again.

</Callout>
