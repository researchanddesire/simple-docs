## Where we ship from

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

All orders ship from our warehouse in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. We don't have a warehouse anywhere else, and we don't offer pickup in Toronto.

</Callout>

## Do you ship to my country?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We ship to most countries. The quickest way to check is to add your products to the cart and enter your address at checkout: you'll see every shipping option and its price for your address before you pay.

A few products can't be shipped to every country. If checkout doesn't offer shipping to your address, email us **before you order** and we'll tell you what's possible.

</Callout>

## Which carrier will deliver my order?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We choose the carrier based on your address and what's in your order:

- **United States:** most parcels go with Chit Chats, which hands them to USPS for delivery in the US.
- **Canada:** Canada Post or UPS.
- **Europe and the UK:** DHL Express, UPS or Canada Post.
- **Everywhere else:** DHL, UPS or Canada Post.

The Chastity Lockbox and the Deepthroat Trainer have built-in batteries, so they have fewer carrier options. Outside North America they usually travel with DHL or UPS. We can't always use the carrier you prefer.

</Callout>

## Addresses that can cause problems

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

- **PO boxes:** fine in the US and Canada.
- **Parcel lockers and pickup stations** (for example Packstation): UPS often refuses them. Use a street address if you can.
- **Parcel forwarders:** allowed. If your forwarder needs an invoice, email us and we'll send one.
- **US "General Delivery":** we can't ship to it.
- **Apartment or unit numbers:** double-check them. Shop Pay, PayPal and Google Pay sometimes fill in an old address.

To change your address after you order, see [Cancel or change an order](/shop/cancel-or-change-an-order#change-your-shipping-address).

</Callout>

## How long does delivery take?

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Each shipping option at checkout shows the carrier's typical delivery time in business days, counted from the day your parcel leaves us. Add that to the time your order takes to ship: see [Order processing and ship times](/shop/order-processing-and-ship-times).

Strikes, customs checks and tariff changes can add time we don't control. If that happens on your route, we'll tell you and, where we can, send your order with another carrier.

</Callout>
