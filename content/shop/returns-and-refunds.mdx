## Our returns policy

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

"Because of the nature of our products we do not allow returns, but please reach out to us at support@researchanddesire.com and we'll be happy to help!"

We still never want anyone stuck with something they can't use. If your order isn't working out, email us and tell us what's going on. We'll look at what we can do.

</Callout>

## If something is faulty

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

A fault isn't a return. If your product arrived damaged, is missing parts, or stops working, we'll fix it, usually by sending a replacement part or device. Start here:

- [Damaged, missing or lost items](/shop/damaged-missing-or-lost)
- [Warranty and defects](/shop/warranty-and-defects)

</Callout>

## If it isn't what you expected

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Tell us what isn't working for you. Often it's a setup problem we can solve together. If it isn't, our team looks at each case individually.

To avoid surprises, please ask us any questions **before** you order, and remember you can cancel free of charge until your order ships. See [Cancel or change an order](/shop/cancel-or-change-an-order).

</Callout>

## How refunds work

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

- We refund to your **original payment method**. We don't replace a refund with store credit.
- Card refunds usually appear within **5–10 business days**. PayPal refunds can take up to 10 business days.
- We refund the amount you paid us. We can't refund import fees or carrier charges you paid to someone else.

</Callout>

## Before you open a dispute

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

If something has gone wrong with your order, please email us first. We aim to reply to anything about a bank or PayPal dispute within one business day, and it's usually the fastest way to get your money back.

</Callout>

## Your rights

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under the consumer laws where you live.

</Callout>
