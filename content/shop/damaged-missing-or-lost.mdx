Sorry if your order didn't arrive the way it should have. Here's what to check and what to send us so we can fix it quickly.

## Check the box first

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Kits are packed in several bags, and small parts are sometimes packed with bigger ones. Before you contact us:

1. Open every bag and check the bottom of the box.
2. For OSSM kits, compare what you have with the [OSSM bill of materials](https://ohai.researchanddesire.com/ossm/bom).
3. Some older build videos show parts that kits no longer include. For example, the PitClamp Mini replaced the older pivot body.

</Callout>

## Something is missing or wrong

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

1. Lay out everything you received and take one photo of it all.
2. Email the photo to [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with your order number and what's missing or wrong.
3. If something is missing, we ship it to you free, with tracking.
4. If you received a wrong or extra item, keep it. You don't need to send it back.

For a small part going outside Canada and the US, import fees can cost more than the part. In that case we may offer you a refund for the part instead.

</Callout>

## Something arrived damaged

<Callout type="warn" title="Safety first">

- If a power supply, cable, board, motor or battery arrived damaged, don't plug it in, charge it or switch it on.
- If anything sparks, smokes, smells of burning, gets hot or has a swollen battery, unplug it or switch it off when it's safe to reach, and stop using it.
- Don't use a machine with a cracked frame, stand or mount, even if it seems to work.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

1. Keep the box and the packing material.
2. Take photos of the damaged part and of the outside of the box.
3. Email the photos to [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with your order number.

If a part is broken, we send you a free replacement part and any hardware you need to fit it. If the damage affects how safely the device works, we send a replacement device. You don't need to send the damaged item back.

</Callout>

## Your parcel hasn't arrived

### Tracking hasn't changed

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Parcels can go several days without a new scan, especially at customs or when a parcel moves from one carrier to another. If your tracking hasn't changed for **5 business days**, email us your order number. We'll check with the carrier, open a trace if it's needed, and tell you when to expect the next update.

</Callout>

### Tracking says delivered, but you don't have it

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

1. Allow up to 2 business days. Carriers sometimes scan a parcel as delivered before it arrives.
2. Check around your door and mailbox, with your neighbours, and in your building's mailroom or reception.
3. Check the delivery address in your order confirmation email.
4. If you still can't find it, email us your order number. We'll open an investigation with the carrier. If the parcel isn't found, we'll send your order again or refund you.

</Callout>

### Returned to sender

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

If your parcel comes back to us, we'll email you.

- If the carrier made a mistake, we send it again for free, or refund you if you prefer.
- If the address was incomplete or the parcel wasn't collected in time, we'll send it again once it's back with us, and we'll ask you to pay for the new shipping.

</Callout>
