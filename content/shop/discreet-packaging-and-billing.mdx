We know privacy matters. Here's what other people can see when your order arrives, on your statement, and while you set up your device.

## The box and the label

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

- Every order ships in a **plain brown cardboard box** with no R+D logo, product name or pictures on the outside.
- The sender on the shipping label is **R+D Labs**.
- For international parcels, the customs description is a plain description of electronic parts. It doesn't name the product's purpose.

</Callout>

## Your card or PayPal statement

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Your statement shows **[billing descriptor — AJ to confirm]**. It doesn't name the product you bought.

</Callout>

## When customs needs paperwork

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

If customs or the carrier asks for an invoice, the invoice lists the products in your order. We'll tell you before we send one, so you can decide how to handle it.

</Callout>

## While you set up a device

When you set up Wi-Fi on the device itself, it creates a temporary Wi-Fi network for you to join. While that setup screen is open, anyone nearby who looks at the list of available Wi-Fi networks can see the network's name:

| Device | Setup network name |
| --- | --- |
| Chastity Lockbox | Lockbox Setup |
| Deepthroat Trainer | Trainer Setup |
| OSSM | OSSM Setup |
| OSSM Wireless Remote | RADR Setup |
