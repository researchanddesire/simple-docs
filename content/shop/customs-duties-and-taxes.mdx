Import fees depend on where your parcel is going. This page explains who pays them and what to do if customs or the carrier contacts you.

## Canada and the United States

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

You won't pay import fees when your parcel arrives.

- **Canada:** orders ship within Canada, so there's nothing to import.
- **United States:** we ship duties paid. We cover any duties and tariffs, so the carrier shouldn't ask you for money. If anyone asks you to pay an import charge on an R+D parcel, **don't pay it**. Email us the message and we'll sort it out with the carrier.

</Callout>

## Everywhere else

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

You pay any import VAT or GST, customs duty and the carrier's handling fee when your parcel arrives. Our prices and shipping charges at checkout don't include them, and we can't collect them for you at checkout.

- Your country's customs office decides how much you owe.
- The carrier (for example DHL or UPS) usually collects the amount before delivery and adds its own handling or brokerage fee.
- Once it's paid, the carrier delivers as normal.

If you're not sure what you'd owe, check with your national customs office before you order.

</Callout>

## Customs or the carrier asked for documents

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Sometimes customs or the carrier asks for an invoice, a product description or another form before it releases your parcel.

1. Forward the full message to [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com), including any deadline and upload link.
2. We'll send you the documents, or submit them for you, as quickly as we can.

An invoice lists the products in your order, so it's less discreet than the parcel itself.

</Callout>

## What we can't do

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

We declare what's in the parcel and what you paid for it. We can't mark an order as a gift, lower its declared value, or split it into several parcels to avoid import fees.

</Callout>

## If you don't want to pay the fees

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

You can refuse the delivery. Before you do, please know what happens next:

1. The carrier sends the parcel back to us. This can take several weeks, and sometimes up to 3 months.
2. When it arrives, we refund the price of your products to your original payment method.
3. We can't refund the original shipping cost, or any fees the carrier has already charged you.

</Callout>

## If customs seizes your parcel

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Email us the customs notice. We'll look at what happened and, if the parcel can't be delivered, refund your order.

</Callout>

## Replacement parts we send you

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

If we send you a free replacement or a missing part and the carrier charges you import fees, keep the receipt and email it to us. We'll pay you back.

</Callout>
