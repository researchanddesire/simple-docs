# Before the session

1. Confirm the backboard, suction cups, trainer, and toy are secure.
2. Choose the correct toy in **Deepthroat Trainer → Toybox**.
3. Unplug the charging cable.
4. Make sure you can stop and move away from the trainer immediately.

# Begin

1. Open **Deepthroat Trainer** in the [R+D Dashboard](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com/).
2. Choose a short, simple training template, such as a short Repetition or Endurance session.
3. Review the session settings.
4. Press **Start** when you are ready.

For early sessions, use one to three short segments. Increase only one setting at a time after you can complete the current session comfortably.

Keep your breathing steady and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness, or distress. Do not continue a session to improve a score.

After the session, clean the toy and wipe the trainer and mount as described in the product care instructions.
