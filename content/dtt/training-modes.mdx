# Choose a template

Training templates organize a session into timed segments with different targets. Open **Deepthroat Trainer** in the Dashboard to see the templates currently available to your account.

For a first session, choose the shortest and simplest template available. Review its duration and targets before pressing **Start**.

# Modes

- **Repetition:** follow a moving target through a controlled insertion and return to the start position. It is the simplest place to learn a repeatable rhythm.
- **Bounce:** control the pace in both directions. The insertion and withdrawal are evaluated equally, so begin more slowly than in Repetition.
- **Endurance:** move into a target depth and hold it for the chosen duration. The timer advances while you remain in the target zone.
- **Freeform:** choose your own pace, depth, and rhythm while working toward an average repetitions-per-minute target.

Start with shallow depth, slow movement, and short duration. Change one target at a time between sessions.

# During a session

Follow the current segment shown in the Dashboard. Focus on controlled movement and steady breathing, not points or leaderboard position.

Stop the session whenever you need to. Training results are optional feedback; they are not a reason to continue through discomfort.
