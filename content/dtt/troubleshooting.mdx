# The mount moves

Stop the session. Remove the backboard, clean and dry both the suction cups and mounting surface, then reinstall it on smooth non-porous material. Do not train until it remains firm when checked by hand.

# Pairing or Wi-Fi fails

- Charge and power on the trainer.
- Keep it near the router during setup.
- Use a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
- Retry from **Settings → Devices** using the Trainer ID shown by the device.

# A session or result is missing

Confirm the trainer is online and open **Deepthroat Trainer → Recent Activity**. An offline session must reconnect and upload before it appears or awards points.

If a problem continues, email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with the Trainer ID and the exact step that failed.
