title: "E-DTT-1: Online Check Failed"
description: "Diagnose a Trainer that joined Wi-Fi but cannot complete the R+D online health check."
---

## Meaning

E-DTT-1 means the online health check failed. The Trainer may have joined the local Wi-Fi network, but it did not complete the required server request. This does not prove that a firewall is the cause.

## Diagnostic ladder

### 1. Confirm ordinary network service

Using another device on the same Wi-Fi, confirm that the internet works. Restart the router only if you are authorized to do so and other clients also have a problem.

### 2. Repeat Wi-Fi setup

Follow [Connect the Trainer to Wi-Fi](/dtt/quick-start/pairing/wifi-setup). Re-enter the password and let the Trainer restart.

### 3. Check network restrictions

Look for a captive browser login, client isolation, paused/blocked devices, parental controls, DNS filtering, or an IoT/guest network that blocks outbound traffic. Do not disable the entire firewall; change only a rule you understand or ask the network administrator.

### 4. Compare with a simple hotspot

Connect the Trainer to a phone hotspot as a temporary diagnostic test. If the hotspot works, the original network configuration is the likely boundary. If both fail, collect evidence for support.

<Callout type="info">

A hotspot test can use mobile data. Restore the intended network afterward; do not leave credentials on a shared phone without permission.

</Callout>

## Success

The Trainer restarts, completes preflight without E-DTT-1, and fetches the expected template and toy.
