## Charge

1. Switch the Trainer off and place it on a dry, stable surface.
2. Inspect the USB-C port and cable. Do not connect power when either is wet or damaged.
3. Connect a known-good USB-C cable, then connect its other end to a working USB power source.
4. Confirm that the display shows a charging or battery indication.
5. Disconnect the cable before mounting or training.

![USB-C charging port on top of the Deep Throat Trainer](<../img/image (4) (1) (1) (1).webp>)

<Callout type="warn">

Stop charging and disconnect power if the device becomes unusually hot, swollen, odorous, leaking, or visibly damaged. Move it away from combustible material only when safe and contact support.

</Callout>

## If no charging indication appears

1. Verify the outlet or USB port with another device.
2. Try another known-good USB-C cable and power source.
3. Inspect the Trainer port without inserting tools.
4. Leave a deeply discharged unit connected for a while, then check the display again. No exact recovery or full-charge time is published in this repository.
5. If it powers only while connected or immediately shuts down after disconnecting, stop use and contact support.

## Published limits

The repository does not provide a verified input rating, charge time, runtime, capacity, storage-charge percentage, or user battery-replacement procedure. Use only undamaged conventional USB power equipment and ask [support@researchanddesire.com](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us) for hardware-specific limits.

<Callout type="success" title="Ready for setup">

The Trainer boots while disconnected from USB and the display shows the Trainer ID. Continue to [Charge and mount](/dtt/quick-start/getting-started).

</Callout>
