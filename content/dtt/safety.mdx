---
title: "Safety and Stopping"
description: "How to stop a Deep Throat Trainer session at any moment, mount the Trainer securely, and know when to stop."
---

The Trainer measures how far you are from its sensor and gives you feedback. It doesn't move, and it can't sense your breathing or how you feel. You decide when to stop, every time.

<Callout type="warning" title="Practice safely">

If you feel pain, nausea, dizziness, or difficulty breathing, stop immediately and rest. Train slowly, use plenty of water-based lubricant, and listen to your body.

</Callout>

## Stop at any moment

1. Pull back and move away from the toy. You don't need to press anything.
2. If you use a vibrating accessory: in Repetition, Endurance and Bounce segments it fades out within about 1 second after you leave the target zone. In Freeform it follows your pace, so it can take longer to stop.
3. When you can reach it safely, slide the power switch down to turn the Trainer off, and unplug the USB cable if it's connected.

<Callout type="info" title="The button doesn't stop a segment">

During training the button does nothing, so you can't stop a segment by pressing it by mistake, and you can't stop it with the button either. Pulling back always works. Repetition, Endurance and Bounce segments can't finish while you're pulled back, and they have no time limit, so take as long as you need. A Freeform segment runs for its set time.

</Callout>

## Before every session

1. Mount the backboard on a clean, smooth, non-porous surface such as glass, acrylic or glazed tile. Don't use painted walls or porous materials.
2. Lightly moisten the suction cups, press firmly, and check that the board is secure. Test the suction before every use.
3. Attach the Trainer to the backboard's hook-and-loop mount. Make sure it can't wobble or shift.
4. Use a suction-cup toy up to 3" (8 cm) in diameter, cleaned according to its material guidelines.
5. Unplug the USB cable before you start a session.
6. If you're new to the Trainer, begin with the lowest difficulty and short segments.

Proposed setup text. None of this comes from firmware or a published page:

- Inspect the Trainer, backboard, suction cups, toy and cable before each session. Don't use anything that is cracked, loose, wet or damaged.

## Calibration and depth targets

- When the Trainer asks you to calibrate, it shows **Put the tip in your mouth and press the button**, then **Go as deep as you can and press the button**. For the second step, press at a depth that is comfortable for you, not the most you can force. The Trainer uses it as your deepest point.
- Some templates move the target deeper than your calibrated depth as a segment goes on. If a target feels too deep, pull back. Then lower the template's **Depth increase**, and set your toy's minimum to **5 cm** or more in your Toybox.
- To start over, open **Toybox** in the dashboard, select your toy and choose **Reset Calibration**. The Trainer asks you to calibrate again in your next session.
- Your grade measures how long you stayed in the target. It isn't a safety limit or a goal to push past.

## Hands-Free Mode

With Hands-Free Mode on (Ultra subscription), the Trainer moves through most screens and its segments without button presses. Calibration still needs the button. Check your template before you start. You can still pull back at any moment.

## Battery and charging

- The Trainer has a built-in lithium battery, glued in place inside the case.
- Use dry, undamaged cables and adapters. Don't charge the Trainer if the port is wet or the cable is damaged.
- The only low-battery sign is the battery icon on the screen, and the Trainer doesn't switch itself off. Turn it off with the power switch when you finish.

## Cleaning

Wipe the Trainer body with a soft microfiber cloth and a mild surface cleaner. Avoid soaking. Clean toys according to their material guidelines. Always disconnect power before cleaning.

Let the Trainer and its USB-C port dry completely before you charge it.

## When to stop

Stop right away and move away from the toy if you feel pain, nausea, dizziness, or difficulty breathing.

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft — needs AJ decision">

Proposed additions, carried from the removed August 2026 draft. They are not from firmware or a published page:

- Also stop for bleeding, numbness, panic, or if the mount or toy moves.
- Get urgent medical help for breathing difficulty, a serious injury, or symptoms that don't go away.
- Don't start again until you know what caused the problem and have fixed it.
- If a partner helps set up your sessions, agree beforehand how either of you can stop. You must always be able to stop and move away yourself.

</Callout>

## Report a safety problem

Switch the Trainer off and unplug it first. Then email [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) with:

- what happened, and when
- the training mode or template, if it happened during a session
- your firmware version, shown at the bottom left of the start-up screen
- photos of the Trainer, the backboard and the charger. Please show only the device.
