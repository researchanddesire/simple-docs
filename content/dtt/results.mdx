# View a completed session

1. Sign in to the [R+D Dashboard](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com/).
2. Open **Deepthroat Trainer**.
3. Select the session under **Recent Activity**.

A result can include an overall grade, points earned, and performance for each segment. Use segment details to understand where accuracy, depth, or time in the target zone changed. A connected partner with access can review the same session details.

# Leaderboards and points

The Dashboard shows the leaderboards and point totals currently available for your account. They are optional progress features and should not override comfort or safety.

Sessions completed while the trainer is offline do not upload or award points until the trainer reconnects and uploads them.
