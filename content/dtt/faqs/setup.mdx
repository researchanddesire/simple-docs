## Where should I start?

Use the [first-session checklist](/dtt/quick-start). It places safety and mounting before pairing, toy setup, calibration, and training.

## Which surfaces work for suction cups?

Use a clean, smooth, non-porous surface such as glass, acrylic, or glazed tile. A surface is unsuitable if a firm pull makes a cup lift or slide. Texture, grout, paint, dust, and residue can prevent a reliable seal.

## Where is the Trainer ID?

It appears near the top of the display during startup. Restart the Trainer if it passes before you can copy it. See [Pairing](/dtt/quick-start/pairing/pairing-guide).

## Pairing succeeded. Why is the Trainer offline?

Pairing links the hardware to an account; it does not provide network credentials. Complete [Wi-Fi setup](/dtt/quick-start/pairing/wifi-setup).

## Why does the installed toy matter in the dashboard?

The selected toy supplies context for position and calibration. The installed toy and selected toy must match. See [Toy setup](/dtt/quick-start/using-toys).

## When should I recalibrate?

Calibrate when the device prompts, after changing the physical toy, after changing the mounting geometry, or after resetting a toy’s calibration. Calibration should reflect a comfortable controlled range, not the greatest possible range.

## What confirms setup is complete?

The mount passes a pull test, the charging cable is disconnected, the paired device appears in the correct account, the online check succeeds when Wi-Fi is required, the installed and selected toys match, and calibration accepts both positions.
