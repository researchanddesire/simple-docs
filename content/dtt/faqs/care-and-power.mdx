## How do I charge it?

Use a dry, undamaged USB-C cable and power source. Confirm a charging indication on the display. Disconnect the cable before training. See [Charging](/dtt/specs/charging).

## How long does charging take or the battery last?

The current repository does not contain a verified charge-time, capacity, runtime, or battery-storage specification. Environment, accessories, firmware, and battery condition can change runtime, so this guide does not publish an estimate.

## It does not turn on. What should I check?

Verify the outlet or USB port with another device, try a known-good data/power cable, inspect the port for damage or moisture, and allow a deeply discharged unit time on power before retrying. If it works only while connected or becomes hot, swollen, odorous, or visibly damaged, disconnect it if safe and stop using it.

## Can I replace the battery?

No user battery replacement procedure or approved part specification is present in this repository. Contact support rather than substituting a battery.

## How often should I inspect it?

Before every use. No additional fixed service interval is published. Inspect the mount, attachment, housing, controls, sensor area, USB port, and toy.

## How should I store it?

Store it switched off, dry, protected from impact, and without a toy attached. Do not put weight on the display, button, sensor, or connector.
