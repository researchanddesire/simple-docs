## Which mode should I choose?

Use the [training mode comparison](/dtt/training-modes). Repetition follows repeated target motion, Endurance measures time in a target zone, Freeform follows a pace target, and Bounce tracks controlled motion in both directions.

## What is a template?

A template is a saved sequence of segments. Confirm its toy, segment types, and active status before downloading it. Imported and partner-created templates require the same review as your own.

## What does a grade mean?

A grade summarizes how the recorded movement matched that segment’s target. Different modes measure different behavior, so a percentage is not a universal skill or safety score.

## How do points work?

Points are calculated from session data accepted by the dashboard. Mode-specific inputs differ and product logic can change. See [Results, leaderboards, and points](/dtt/quick-start/checking-results#leaderboards-and-points) and the current dashboard display.

## Why is a completed session missing?

Confirm the correct account and device, then verify the session was online. The server creates results after receiving a completion request. Because the checked-out repository lacks DTT firmware, a durable offline queue, retention capacity, and retry timing cannot be verified. Treat an offline session as unsaved until it appears in the dashboard.

## Why are template controls disabled?

Check which account you are controlling, the partner-control state, Give Up Control settings, and the subscription attached to the person performing the edit. Use the dashboard’s current message as the authority.

## Can I opt out of public leaderboards?

The current application source does not expose a public-leaderboard opt-out. This documentation therefore does not promise one. Email support if you need current account-visibility guidance.
