## What the system does

The Trainer measures your position relative to a calibrated toy and displays feedback for the active segment. The dashboard stores templates and shows results that successfully reach the server. Pairing links a physical Trainer to an account; Wi-Fi is separate and supplies the network connection.

<Callout type="info" title="Pairing and Wi-Fi are different steps">

Pairing establishes account ownership. Wi-Fi lets the Trainer fetch settings, send results, and check for updates. Completing one does not complete the other.

</Callout>
