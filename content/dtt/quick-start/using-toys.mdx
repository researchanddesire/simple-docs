The Trainer uses the selected toy’s dimensions and calibration when interpreting position. A mismatched physical toy and dashboard selection can make feedback inaccurate.

## Compatibility checklist

Use a toy only when all of these are true:

- its manufacturer identifies the material and cleaning method;
- it is clean, intact, and free of tears, cracks, sharp edges, tackiness, or other deterioration;
- it can attach securely without an improvised loose adapter;
- its shape leaves the Trainer’s sensor path unobstructed;
- you can use and remove it while remaining in control of your movement.

The repository does not publish universal minimum or maximum toy dimensions. If fit or sensor clearance is uncertain, do not force it; contact support before use.

## Lubricant and cleaning

Follow the toy manufacturer’s lubricant compatibility. For R+D silicone toys, the existing product guidance specifies water-based lubricant. Do not assume a lubricant is compatible with an unknown material.

Clean and fully dry the toy before and after use. Do not immerse the Trainer body.

## Install the toy

1. Switch the Trainer off.
2. Inspect the attachment point and toy.
3. Attach the toy using its intended connection.
4. Pull-test it gently. It must not rotate, loosen, or detach.
5. Confirm that nothing blocks the sensor.

## Assign it in the dashboard

1. Sign in and open your paired Trainer’s **Toybox**.
2. Select the matching R+D toy, or add a custom toy using measurements you can verify.
3. Set that entry as the default, or assign it to the specific template you will use.
4. Confirm that the device session shows the expected toy before starting.

## When you change toys

Update the dashboard assignment and recalibrate when prompted. Do not reuse calibration merely because two toys look similar.

Continue to [Calibration](/dtt/technical/calibration).
