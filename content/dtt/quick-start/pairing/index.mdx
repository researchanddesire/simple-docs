---
title: "Pairing Your Trainer"
---

Pairing links your trainer to your dashboard account. This enables you to track your training history and receive personalized settings. Wi-Fi is optional for pairing but required for syncing features.

Follow the guide below to pair your Trainer:

<Callout type="info" title="To pair your device you must have:">

1. A R+D dashboard account
2. A Deepthroat Trainer that is connected to Wi-Fi

</Callout>


<Steps>
  <Step>

### Log in to your R+D dashboard account

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Select "My Devices"

<img src="./images/lkbx-pairing-settings-dropdown-annotated.webp" alt="lkbx pairing settings dropdown annotated" width="600" height="575" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Select "Pair a Deepthroat Trainer"

<img src="./images/pair-a-trainer-unannotated.webp" alt="pair a trainer unannotated" width="371" height="119" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Turn on your Deepthroat Trainer

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Take note of the Trainer ID that the device screen displays on boot

<img src="./images/trainer-pairing-id-annotated.webp" alt="trainer pairing id annotated" width="600" height="900" />

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Enter your Trainer ID in the "Deepthroat Trainer ID" field

<img src="./images/pairing-code-entry-annotated.webp" alt="pairing code entry annotated" width="600" height="275" />


  </Step>
  <Step>

### Select "Pair device"

<img src="./images/pair-device-annotated.webp" alt="pair device annotated" width="600" height="274" />

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success">

Your Trainer is now paired to your account!

</Callout>
