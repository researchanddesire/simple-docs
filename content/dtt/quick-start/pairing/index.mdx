## Which task do you need?

| Goal                                      | Guide                                                      | Success state                              |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Link the physical Trainer to your account | [Pair the Trainer](/dtt/quick-start/pairing/pairing-guide) | it appears under your dashboard devices    |
| Give the Trainer internet access          | [Connect to Wi-Fi](/dtt/quick-start/pairing/wifi-setup)    | it restarts and completes its online check |

Pairing controls account ownership and access. Wi-Fi supplies connectivity for settings, results, and update checks. One can fail while the other remains configured.

<Callout type="info">

Create an account at [dashboard.researchanddesire.com](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com/?register=true) before pairing.

</Callout>

## Transfer or account-access problem

The current owner should unpair the Trainer before transfer. If the owner cannot be reached or the original account is inaccessible, do not repeatedly claim the device. Email [support@researchanddesire.com](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us) with the Trainer ID and available purchase details.
