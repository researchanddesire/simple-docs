---

## After each use

1. Switch the Trainer off and disconnect USB power.
2. Remove the toy.
3. Clean the toy according to its manufacturer’s material guidance.
4. Wipe the Trainer housing with a soft, lightly damp cloth and mild surface cleaner.
5. Let every part dry before storage or charging.

<Callout type="warn">

Do not immerse the Trainer, run it under water, or let liquid enter the display, button, switch, sensor, or USB-C port.

</Callout>

## Inspect before use

Check the housing, sensor area, button, switch, USB-C port, backboard, hook-and-loop attachment, suction cups, and toy connection. Stop using the affected part if it is cracked, loose, corroded, wet, or otherwise damaged.

No replacement threshold or service interval is published in this repository. Inspect before every use and contact support when wear changes how a part holds, charges, or operates.

## Store

Store the Trainer switched off, dry, and protected from impact. Keep the toy detached and store it according to its material guidance. Do not place weight on the display, button, sensor, or USB connector.

## Battery and charging

Use a dry, undamaged USB-C cable and power source. The available product source does not publish an exact charge time, battery chemistry, storage-charge percentage, or replacement procedure, so this guide does not supply those values. See [Charging](/dtt/specs/charging) for the documented indicators and troubleshooting steps.

## Support

For a loose mount, damaged port, battery problem, or replacement part, stop using the device and email [support@researchanddesire.com](https://www.researchanddesire.com/pages/contact-us).
