# Create an account

1. Open the [R+D Dashboard](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com/).
2. Choose **Register**.
3. Continue with your email and password, or use one of the available sign-in providers.
4. If you register by email, open the confirmation message from `app@researchanddesire.com` and confirm your address.
5. Return to the Dashboard and finish the short onboarding steps.

## Sign in without your password

On the sign-in screen, choose **Email link**. Enter your email address, then open the link sent to your inbox.

## Change your password

After signing in, go to **Settings → My Profile → Change Password**.

If the email does not arrive, check spam or junk mail and confirm that you entered the address used for your account.
