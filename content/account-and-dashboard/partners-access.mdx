# Add a partner

1. Sign in to the [R+D Dashboard](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com/).
2. Open **Settings → Partners**.
3. Create an invitation and choose the access you want to request or share.
4. Send the invitation link to your partner.
5. Your partner opens the link and accepts the invitation.

The Dashboard distinguishes between:

- **Give them access to my devices** — lets your partner use supported controls for your devices.
- **Request access to their devices** — asks your partner to share supported controls with you.

Only share device access with someone you trust. Features such as **Give Up Control** can transfer significant control to a partner. Agree on limits and an emergency plan before using them.

You can review or remove partner access from **Settings → Partners**.
