# Pair a device

1. Sign in to the [R+D Dashboard](https://dashboard.researchanddesire.com/).
2. Open **Settings → Devices**.
3. Choose the option to pair your product.
4. Keep the product powered on and nearby, then follow the instructions shown for that product.

Some products pair automatically in a supported browser. Others show an ID or code that you enter in the Dashboard. Use the product guide for the exact steps:

- [Pair an OSSM](/ossm/get-started)
- [Pair a Lockbox](/lockbox/get-started)
- [Pair a Deepthroat Trainer](/dtt/get-started)

## Manage a paired device

Return to **Settings → Devices** to see devices linked to your account. Select a device to view the actions currently available for it.

If pairing fails, confirm that the product is powered on, close any other controller connected to it, and retry from the product's troubleshooting page.
