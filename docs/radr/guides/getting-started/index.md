# Getting Started With RADR

Start here if you are setting up RADR for the first time.

RADR is designed to connect quickly to supported devices, including OSSM and compatible Bluetooth toys. The quickest path is to charge RADR, confirm your target device is ready, then follow the Quick Start.

## Recommended First Step

[Open the Quick Start](/radr/guides/getting-started/quick-start/)

## Before You Begin

- Charge RADR with a USB-C cable.
- Power on the device you want to control.
- Update OSSM firmware if you are pairing with OSSM.
- Close any phone apps that may already be connected to the Bluetooth toy.
