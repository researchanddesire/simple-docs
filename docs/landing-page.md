# Welcome to OSSM!
The OSSM (Open Source Sex Machine) is the kind of product that does what it says on the tin - it's a sex machine with open source hardware and software for any interested parties to to contribute to. The OSSM is a true all-rounder - it is lightweight and portable thanks to its collapsibility, but strong enough to get the job done.
